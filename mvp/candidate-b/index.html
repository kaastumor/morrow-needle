What this prototype does

  1. You provide a known standard.No applicability discovery.
  2. Needle projects its frozen OJ-reference state.Cited, restricted, not cited, and scheduled transitions.
  3. You can inspect the official evidence.EUR-Lex remains authoritative.

The current build is a product-readiness experiment using frozen official evidence. It is designed to test the shape of a narrow standards-status tool, not to act as a current compliance database.
