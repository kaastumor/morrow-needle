How to read this corpus

DERIVATION cases helped discover or explain a failure mechanism. They are useful for explanation and regression, but are not fresh blind validation of that same mechanism. EVALUATION cases were used to test a pre-existing claim under the corpus evaluation protocol.

SURFACED_TRAP_ADJUDICATION means the task identified or strongly cued the dangerous distinction and tested whether it was resolved correctly. LATENT_TRAP_DETECTION means the realistic task did not identify the hidden failure mechanism and tested whether it was noticed before a consequential answer.

Exposure warning: public cases are exposed evidence. They must not be treated as fresh blind evidence; each case’s exposure and reuse state remains visible in its detail view.
