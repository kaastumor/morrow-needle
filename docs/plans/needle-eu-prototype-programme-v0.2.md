# Needle EU — bounded prototype programme

Date: 29 September 2026. Sponsor direction supersedes the earlier landing-page phase limits. This plan is a delivery contract, not a second live queue; [BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md) owns mode, WIP and next action.

## Outcome and finish line

A reader can find covered EU legislation, understand its purpose and relevance, inspect represented acts and their important dates and relationships, then verify consequential statements against official sources. Coverage is explicit and deliberately small. The accepted #489 editorial visual language remains the baseline.

**Prototype v1 is ready for sponsor review** when the bounded content set and source/version record are explicit; journeys A–G below work on desktop and narrow screens; there are no inert promised controls or disconnected act routes; material public caveats and official sources remain accessible; automated and actual browser checks are recorded for the reviewed revision; a developer can build and update the content; and the demonstration states limits and maintenance effort. Reviewable code, sponsor acceptance, merge and public release are separate statuses. Only sponsor review can close the prototype milestone.

Sponsor investment authorizes this build. It does not demonstrate user preference, comparative performance or commercial demand. #214, the frozen corpus and LVD test, #327/#366/#377/#392/#395/#407/#411 and the Candidate A/C external-evidence gates remain intact.

## Observable journeys

| Journey | v1 acceptance scenario |
| --- | --- |
| A — Find and enter | From the product entry, browse or search the named covered acts. A query outside coverage returns an honest no-result state and visible coverage limit. A deep link opens its represented page. |
| B — Understand | The short public explanation states purpose, affected people/products, main effects and material exclusions. Expert expands the same facts without contradicting them. |
| C — Inspect acts | Every represented core act opens a substantive detail view using a consistent purpose/scope/dates/selected changes/relationships/sources contract. Links are not decorative. |
| D — Understand time | A reader can inspect source-linked milestones and distinguish adoption, publication, entry into force, application, conditional transition, operation and proposal where relevant. No end is invented; schematic spacing is labelled. |
| E — Understand relationships | A reader can inspect represented nodes and typed links. Editorial grouping, replacement, amendment and implementing/delegated relationships differ in text as well as styling. A proposal remains non-law and selection is explicit. |
| F — Verify and navigate | Material claims have nearby official links in Public mode, evidence dates and unresolved points are visible, and back/deep links work. Link activation and successful retrieval are reported separately. |
| G — Use reliably | Desktop, 390px and 320px, 200% text, keyboard/focus, disclosures, invalid routes and missing-script behavior are exercised on a built revision. Missing data is labelled rather than filled speculatively. |

## Red-team and architecture decision

| Failure pressure | Control and falsifier |
| --- | --- |
| A polished map implies legal completeness or one hierarchy | Caption every selection and relationship type; test whether a reader can identify a missing act and the editorial root. If the map misleads, use a typed list. |
| Public/Expert becomes two truth stores | Keep one shared content contract and public material caveats/sources. If the two levels disagree in review, stop dependent pages and repair the owner. |
| More pages create unsustainable legal upkeep | Bound the acts, retain source/version/evidence dates, rehearse one correction. Narrow content if it cannot be maintained without broad monitoring. |
| Search appears EU-wide | Search only the named local set and show an explicit no-result/coverage message. |
| Tests or screenshots substitute for working journeys | Exercise built routes and browser interactions; distinguish a blocked browser environment, link activation and destination retrieval. |
| The old evidence gate blocks the authorized prototype, or the prototype is mistaken for value proof | Deliver the sponsor-funded artifact; reserve any user/commercial claim for later external evidence. Candidates A/C remain parked until M4 and sponsor direction. |
| New architecture consumes the programme | Existing static HTML/CSS/JS and current build remain the default. Add shared data only where it prevents demonstrated divergence; no framework/database migration by default. |

At M4, briefly revisit the #418 Candidate A/C portfolio review only as a recommendation: what works, what failed or was unnecessary, what changed, what real owner/workflow/incident evidence is missing, and the smallest useful investigation. Do not activate either lane without sponsor direction.
