# Post-#150 Project Health Check — 2026-09-24

Issue: #150  
Decision: **CONTINUE — IDLE BY DESIGN**

## Executive decision

#150 completed the only experiment authorised after Discovery Cycle 1.

It produced one narrow positive transfer result:

> Needle's evaluation-integrity / evidence-closure discipline exposed a fresh,
> mechanically reproducible inconsistency in DELTA v1.1.0's public
> third-party harness/reproducibility contract.

That result is real, but deliberately bounded.

It does **not** establish:

- a general legal-benchmark audit capability;
- superiority over expert benchmark maintainers;
- a benchmark-audit product or service;
- user demand;
- a reason for another benchmark audit;
- invalidity of DELTA's official published leaderboard.

The essential project identity remains:

> **Morrow // Needle = adversarial legal-research corpus + evaluation protocol.**

There is no evidence-backed next task at this moment.

The correct operating state is therefore:

> **CONTINUE the thesis; IDLE the queue.**

## What #150 changed

Before #150, external transferability of the surviving Needle discipline was an
experiment.

#150 supplied one concrete positive instance:

- target had a credible mature QA baseline;
- sample and failure classes were pre-registered;
- known public defects were excluded from fresh credit;
- four tasks were frozen before audit conclusions;
- three selected tasks produced no confirmed F1–F5 defect;
- one corporate-law issue remained only an unconfirmed legal candidate;
- one mechanical F4 issue crossed the pre-registered materiality threshold;
- strong counterevidence narrowed the claim rather than being ignored.

The transfer claim should therefore survive only in revised form:

> **Needle's evaluation-integrity and evidence-closure discipline can transfer
> usefully to external legal evaluation artifacts, particularly around public
> reproducibility/provenance contracts; general distinctiveness beyond strong
> benchmark QA remains unproven.**

That is evidence for the protocol/research identity, not for a broader product.

## Competing identities

### A — corpus + evaluation protocol

Explains the strongest accumulated evidence:

- adversarial cases remain useful research/evaluation objects;
- derivation and validation stay separated;
- contamination/exposure rules remain useful;
- negative/parity results survive;
- pre-registration and strong-comparator discipline continue to falsify
  overclaims;
- #150 shows the evaluation-integrity discipline can transfer outside Needle's
  own corpus at least once.

**Decision: retain as essential identity.**

### B — corpus only

This is smaller, but now too small to explain the demonstrated contribution.

Removing the reusable evaluation protocol would discard:

- pre-registration;
- comparator discipline;
- construct/mode separation;
- exposure handling;
- evidence-closure discipline;
- the exact mechanism that generated the narrow external #150 transfer result.

**Decision: do not simplify this far.**

### C — general benchmark-audit product / service

#150 provides only one bounded documentation/provenance finding.

No evidence establishes:

- recurring advantage;
- user demand;
- adoption;
- commercial or operational need;
- need for durable audit tooling;
- value beyond ordinary expert QA across multiple benchmarks.

**Decision: reject as current project identity.**

### D — corpus + protocol + Explorer

#136 found lower mechanical indirection in a boxed synthetic workflow, but no
human importance/adoption evidence.

**Decision: keep Explorer parked as a frozen derived projection.**

## Relative-value / historical consistency check

The current decision does not reintroduce the old error:

> existing competitor/baseline → no value.

#150's comparator was deliberately strong. The positive F4 result was retained
despite DELTA's mature QA.

The inverse error is also avoided:

> different/found something → product value.

The finding does not earn a product because importance, recurrence, switching,
distribution and system-level value have not been demonstrated.

## Linked-discovery check

```text

    ↓

    ↓
```

The branch is now saturated for current purposes.

Do not do it without a fresh target trigger.

   audit identity;
2. **process creep** — discovery/red-team documentation becoming the work;
3. **research-until-yes** — running more external audits because one succeeded;
4. **product resurrection** — using narrow protocol value to revive broader
   parked surfaces;
5. **idle-state discomfort** — inventing a task because the queue is empty.

The controls are subtraction and explicit triggers, not more governance.

## Re-entry triggers

Work may resume when at least one concrete trigger exists:

1. a fresh falsifiable legal-information question with authoritative evidence;
2. a real independent adversary that may add/challenge a corpus failure
   mechanism;
3. real intended-user Explorer use that makes H-16 decision-relevant;
4. a named external legal-evaluation artifact with concrete integrity/reproduction

   naturally triggers the evidence-closure pack;
6. a direct external/user job where a Needle-specific response has a falsifiable
   relative-value claim.

A linked-search hypothesis alone is not a trigger.
