# Medical-devices regime constellation v0.1

Status: **BOUNDED MACRO PROTOTYPE — RESEARCH VALUE UNPROVEN**

Owner: #457  
Discovery basis: `docs/discovery/issue456-macro-regime-connectedness-2026-09-27.md`

## Purpose

Test whether a dense EU regime can be made easier to understand through a compressed macro/meso view rather than a giant graph.

The first fixture is the MDR/IVDR medical-devices regime.

## Design rule

> The default view must make the regime feel smaller.

The first render therefore contains nine macro objects/families rather than dozens of individual legal acts.

Native disclosure reveals representative child acts.

## What is represented

- three predecessor directives;
- MDR and IVDR sibling core branches;
- joint transition/change acts;
- implementing-measure family;
- delegated-act family;
- EUDAMED/system-operation lane;
- non-binding guidance lane;
- current proposal lane;
- bounded gap/review semantics.

## What is deliberately not represented

- complete medical-device legislation;
- every delegated/implementing act;
- every MDCG guidance document;
- harmonised standards;
- national implementation;
- case law;
- automated legal-gap findings.

## Source boundary

Primary discovery source:
https://health.ec.europa.eu/medical-devices-new-regulations/overview_en

The Commission source remains a strong baseline.

Family counts are navigation summaries from that overview at the 2026-09-27 evidence checkpoint.

## Gap rule

Absence from this prototype means nothing about legal absence.

The prototype distinguishes:
- coverage gap;
- evidence gap;
- review gap;
- structural-gap candidate.

All are research states, not automatic findings that EU law is defective or incomplete.
