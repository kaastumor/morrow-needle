# Issue #377 — direction review after implementation-Q&A null

Date: 2026-09-26  
Mode: **REVIEW — EU LEGAL-STATE ALLOCATION**

## Trigger

Phase 3A returned:

> **IMPLEMENTATION_QA_RESIDUAL_NULL — MATTER_SPECIFIC_PREVALENCE_UNRESOLVED**

Across 16 externally sourced official implementation questions, 12 were text-sufficient,
four required ordinary cross-instrument research, and zero required the stronger
independently moving operative-state residual.

The project must not respond by hand-picking a more favorable matter-specific set.

## Options reviewed

### A — PARK EU prevalence work

This is scientifically defensible.

Phase 2 already establishes that independently moving operative states exist. Phase 3A
shows they did not recur in a generic implementation-Q&A frame.

Parking now would preserve a narrow existence result and avoid result-seeking.

Weakness:

> the main known limitation of Phase 3A is not merely "we wanted more positives"; it is that
> official Q&A channels systematically abstract away most matter facts.

The question whether the residual appears in **actual disputes with factual state** therefore
remains materially different.

### B — DISCOVER a matter-specific external source frame

This path is eligible only if the source exists independently of Needle and can be selected
mechanically before case inspection.

Three candidate source families were checked.

#### Your Europe Advice

This is an excellent conceptual population: personalised EU-law questions from citizens and
businesses, answered by qualified lawyers.

2025 reporting says YEA received 20,863 requests and answered 14,643 eligible cases.

However, the public surface exposes aggregate/trend reports rather than a systematic public
corpus of individual enquiry + answer records. Personal data/confidentiality are intrinsic
to the service.

Result:

> **HIGHLY RELEVANT POPULATION; NO PUBLIC MECHANICAL CASE CORPUS FOUND**

It cannot currently support a reproducible Phase 3B.

#### SOLVIT

SOLVIT handles real cross-border EU-rights problems and publishes examples of solved cases.

However, the public examples are curated success stories rather than a complete, stable
case-level public dataset from which a mechanically reproducible sample can be drawn.

Result:

> **REAL MATTER SIGNAL; PUBLIC EXAMPLE SET TOO CURATED FOR PREVALENCE**

#### CJEU preliminary references

CURIA systematically publishes case records and, for many references, a request document or
summary preserving:

- parties;
- referring court;
- subject matter of the national proceedings;
- material factual/legal background;
- exact questions referred;
- named EU instruments.

The cases are externally generated real disputes and can be selected by case number /
lodging order before reading their content.

Examples at the start of 2026 include C-1/26, C-2/26 and C-3/26 as requests for preliminary
rulings, and C-10/26 (Lidl Magyarország) as a concrete dispute over a Hungarian food-pricing
measure and Regulation (EU) 1308/2013.

This creates a reproducible matter-specific frame.

Major limitation:

> preliminary references are **not representative of ordinary legal work**. They are
> selected because an EU-law interpretive question reached a national court and warranted a
> reference.

Therefore they can support a **contrast test**:

> does the independently moving state residual appear materially more often in contested
> matter-specific EU-law disputes than in generic official implementation Q&A?

They cannot support a general EU-law prevalence estimate.

### C — Commercial contract review now

Strong regulatory-intelligence incumbents remain important.

But the prior review already reached:

> **PUBLIC_CONTRACT_UNCLEAR — NOT GAP_CONFIRMED**

Public marketing/documentation is too shallow to adjudicate whether systems preserve every
state type losslessly. Repeating public-product review now has lower expected information
than one clean scientific contrast.

A serious commercial comparison later needs product access, documentation at greater depth
or users.

### D — Switch immediately to evaluation science

The preserved legal-AI evaluation-science front remains high-value.

But Issue #377 now has one scientifically distinct unresolved contrast with a viable external
sampling mechanism. Completing one bounded contrast is preferable to abandoning the lane
with a known frame confound unresolved.

## Decision

# **RUN_ONE_MATTER_SPECIFIC_CONTRAST_THEN_REVIEW**

This is not a revival of a broad prevalence claim.

The question is:

> In a mechanically selected sample of contested, matter-specific CJEU preliminary-reference
> disputes involving named EU legislation, how often does the operative analysis depend on a
> separately moving state beyond ordinary text/amendment/cross-instrument interpretation?

The result will be compared with the Phase 3A 0/16 implementation-Q&A null.

## Frozen Phase 3B selection rule

Observation date: **2026-09-26**.

Starting from 2026 Court of Justice case numbers in ascending order:

1. consider cases in numerical order beginning at C-1/26;
2. include only a case whose procedure is a **request/reference for a preliminary ruling**;
3. require a public English request document or English Article-98 summary sufficient to
   recover the main-proceeding facts and questions;
4. require the referred questions to turn materially on at least one named EU
   **Regulation, Directive or Decision**;
5. exclude cases whose EU-law question is only Treaty/Charter/general-principles law;
6. take the **first 12 qualifying cases**;
7. do not inspect Needle class fit until state classification is frozen.

This rule is frozen before detailed case classification.

## Classification

For each case:

- `TEXT_SUFFICIENT`
- `CHANGE_HISTORY_SUFFICIENT`
- `ORDINARY_CROSS_INSTRUMENT`
- `INDEPENDENT_OPERATIVE_STATE`
- `INDETERMINATE`

A case counts as `INDEPENDENT_OPERATIVE_STATE` only if:

1. a state external to ordinary parent-text/amendment/cross-instrument interpretation must
   be resolved for the practical legal analysis;
2. that state can change independently;
3. omitting or projecting it can change the practical answer;
4. the need arises from the real main-proceeding facts, not from Needle labels.

## Stop rule

After 12 qualifying cases:

- preserve the result regardless of direction;
- no replacement cases;
- no extension because the count is "almost informative";
- no class/corpus growth inside the run;
- perform a direction review before any further EU prevalence work.
