# Issue #409 — MDR accessible full-condition incumbent correction

Date: 2026-09-26

## Trigger

After the MDR operative-eligibility replay was frozen but **before execution**, a materially
stronger accessible incumbent was found.

Public V5 Ultimate material currently claims its EU MDR medical-device workflow:

- tracks MDR legacy-device transition deadlines by class;
- tracks Article 120 transition conditions per device, including:
  - no significant change;
  - active MDR application;
  - QMS in place;
  - appropriate surveillance;
- maintains EUDAMED-ready actor/device/certificate data;
- offers self-serve/free-trial access with no card and no mandatory sales call;
- provides an Express usage-based tier with no minimum.

Sources:

- https://v5ultimate.com/eu-mdr-software
- https://v5ultimate.com/faq
- https://v5ultimate.com/company/onboarding

These are vendor claims, not independently verified product performance.

But they are sufficient to invalidate the internal experiment assumption:

> **the strong lean baseline lacks an accessible product that already maintains the Article 120
> condition set.**

## Consequence

The frozen MDR workflow replay is:

# **RETIRED_UNEXECUTED — ACCESSIBLE FULL-CONDITION INCUMBENT FOUND BEFORE EXECUTION**

Running Candidate B against an ordinary spreadsheet/checklist would now be a strawman.

This is not evidence that V5 is correct, complete or appropriate for every SME.

It is evidence that the next MDR question, if pursued, is a **product-capability/access test**:

> does the accessible product actually maintain the cross-owner transition conclusion safely on a
> frozen workload?

That requires direct product access / trial behavior, not more public absence inference.

## Candidate-B methodological implication

Candidate B's original accessibility hypothesis has now survived two incumbent-strengthening
corrections:

- CBAM: low-cost vertical point solutions;
- MDR: self-serve platform explicitly claiming the full Article 120 transition-condition job.

Therefore the project must not define 'lean team' as 'manual spreadsheet by assumption'.

Affordable/self-serve products count as the baseline whenever they exist.
