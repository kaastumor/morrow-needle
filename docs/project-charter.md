# Morrow // Needle — Project charter

Status: **CANONICAL PROJECT NORTH STAR**

This charter owns durable purpose, contribution and project boundaries.

It does **not** own the current issue, corpus count, research mode or next task.
Those mutable facts belong to `BACKLOG.md`, the accepted corpus index and the active
issue/PR.

## Purpose

Morrow // Needle is an **adversarial legal-research known-failure reference corpus +
exposed regression fixtures + minimal evaluation discipline for EU legal change**.

Its demonstrated project-specific contribution is the disciplined preservation of:

- real legal-information traps;
- the decisive consequential failure each case exposes;
- source/evidence lineage;
- exposure and reuse status;
- negative and parity evidence;
- reproducible evaluation boundaries when a comparative claim genuinely warrants them.

Needle is not supported as a proprietary legal-reasoning method or a general
corpus-assisted diagnostic advantage.

## Current project form

The smallest supported form is:

> **corpus + evidence references + minimal evaluation discipline**

The canonical corpus entry point is `corpus/index-v0.1.json`.

The corpus as a whole is a **known-failure reference corpus**. Executable regression status is
case/fixture-specific and requires a preserved runnable task plus an expected/evaluator
contract; exposure does not disqualify a case from regression reuse, but a failure record
without that contract is reference/debugging material rather than a turnkey regression
test.

The primary external reference contract is **legal failure analysis / evaluation design /
debugging**, not routine assistance while answering an ordinary legal question. The
repository evidence estate preserves the decisive legal-state distinction, evidence
lineage, provenance/exposure and accepted boundaries needed to reconstruct and challenge a
known failure. A compact release/navigation layer may point to that evidence without
becoming the evidence itself.

The canonical comparative-evaluation protocol is
`docs/evaluations/adversarial-corpus-protocol-v0.1.md`, used only when a new claim earns
a sealed comparison. It is not a standing experiment engine.

Needle Method may be used as a structured handoff/reporting convention. Canonical
persistence, software projections and higher product surfaces are optional and must earn
use on a concrete task.

## North star

> Given a concrete EU legal-information question, first solve it with the strongest
> defensible research method. Preserve durable Needle state only when it materially
> improves provenance, historical reconstruction, repeatability, regression value or
> later inspection.

The preferred order is:

1. strongest boring source-grounded baseline;
2. existing corpus/reference material where relevant;
3. optional structured Method dossier when handoff/reporting benefits;
4. persistence or software only when the concrete job earns it.

The target is safer, inspectable and reproducible understanding at minimum durable
complexity — not processed-document volume, feature count, class count, ontology coverage
or project activity.

## Strong incumbent baseline

Needle must compare claims against the strongest realistic alternative for the job,
including as appropriate:

- authentic/official EU and national sources;
- mature legal doctrine and established research methods;
- mature legal version/change tools;
- ordinary source-linked notes and direct reading;
- a capable source-grounded model with comparable access.

Existing doctrine or a competitor is neither automatic rejection nor validation.

For corpus classification:

> **Existing is not useless. Different is not valuable. Novel is not demanded.**

A separate class must preserve a pre-specified consequential regression distinction, not
merely doctrinal or descriptive nuance.

## Scientific status

The project's strongest comparative gates narrowed Needle repeatedly.

The sealed #214 index-assisted latent diagnostic pilot produced:

> **0 diagnostic rescues / 0 C-only regressions**

Under its frozen rule, a general latent corpus-workflow advantage was not demonstrated.
Do not retry, reinterpret or rescue that null with harder post-hoc cases or secondary
metrics.

The corpus remains useful as exposed regression/reference material. That value does not
itself establish product demand, workflow superiority or model-performance improvement.

## Sponsor continuity

The sponsor funds an active programme and rejects `IDLE BY DESIGN` as the default
project state.

Continuity means **purposeful operation**, not an automatic experiment conveyor.

A completed horizon may transition to exactly one bounded mode:

- **DISCOVER** — investigate a consequential unresolved question;
- **CONSOLIDATE** — reconcile, compress, retire or simplify accumulated state;
- **USE** — apply accepted assets to a real task within their supported scope;
- **REVIEW / RELEASE** — assess or prepare a coherent finished deliverable;
- **MAINTAIN** — preserve correctness, evidence validity, integrity, cost or operability.

The current mode and WIP are owned only by `BACKLOG.md`.

Stopping a hypothesis, stopping expansion, completing a deliverable and stopping the
entire sponsored programme are different decisions.

## Complexity rule

For correctness, validity and canonical representation, new durable complexity requires a
demonstrated failure of the simpler owner.

For product/workflow surfaces under correctness parity, complexity may instead earn itself
through observed meaningful relative value for a real job, provided evidence integrity,
provenance, uncertainty handling and correctness do not regress.

Historical code, schemas, workflows and projections do not remain active merely because
they exist.

A historical surface is **passive by default** unless it is required by:

- the current project identity;
- a current corpus evidence owner;
- an authorised live/release gate; or
- an explicitly reactivated task.

Passive artifacts remain preserved but create no automatic expansion or maintenance
obligation.

## Public-product status

Broad public-product expansion remains **STOPPED, not deleted**.

Existing Explorer, Thread, X-Ray/dependency-ripple, Retrieval, Source Anomaly, Half-Life,
feed/card and operational artifacts are preserved research history. They may re-enter only
when a concrete task or observed user/research job independently earns the surface.

Unused architecture is not evidence of demand.

## Durable epistemic boundary

The binding epistemic invariants are scoped in `docs/foundation-v0.1.md`.

At minimum:

- appropriate evidence outranks model inference;
- source roles and evidence classes remain explicit;
- legal text, interpretation, legal effect, procedure and source state do not collapse;
- time and territorial/scope state remain as precise as the question requires;
- uncertainty and abstention survive;
- provenance and exposure remain auditable;
- derived views do not become competing legal truth stores.

## Governance ownership

One concern has one live owner:

- **purpose / non-goals:** this charter;
- **epistemic/source invariants:** `docs/foundation-v0.1.md`;
- **delivery/discovery execution rules:** `docs/way-of-working.md`;
- **current mode, sole WIP and immediate priority:** `BACKLOG.md`;
- **frozen task criteria and pending evidence:** active issue + PR;
- **accepted cases/classes:** `corpus/index-v0.1.json`;
- **project-thesis evidence:** `docs/value-evidence.md`;
- **material live beliefs:** `docs/assumptions.md`.

Other audits, result documents, plans and decision records are evidence/history unless a
live owner explicitly incorporates them.

Do not duplicate mutable current state into this charter.

## Reopening rules

A stopped hypothesis or passive surface may reopen when a material boundary changes, for
example:

- new official/source evidence contradicts an accepted answer;
- a real task exposes a consequential failure;
- intended use changes materially;
- comparator capability changes enough to invalidate the previous decision boundary;
- a new case breaks a class boundary;
- real repeated use creates a new workflow/value claim.

Do not reopen merely because another example can be found.

## Explicit non-goals

- a general-interest EU legal-news feed;
- feature accumulation to create a moat;
- personalised legal recommendations;
- replacing lawyers or issuing legal advice;
- predicting political or judicial outcomes;
- broad jurisdiction expansion by default;
- opaque importance/risk scoring;
- a general-purpose legal knowledge graph;
- infrastructure for hypothetical scale;
- custom model training;
- taxonomy growth as a project objective.

## Repository discipline

GitHub is the canonical project record, not an operational database.

Deterministic CI and sanitation protect repository integrity; they are not scientific
evidence. Live probes remain observation tools rather than canonical truth.

Branch protection/ruleset settings remain **UNVERIFIED** whenever repository
administration access is unavailable.
