# Issue #86 — Strong boring baseline results

**Protocol stage:** completed before Needle-specific contract mapping.

The result is deliberately uncomfortable for Full Needle:

**the strong baseline answered all five cases correctly.**

That means the remaining identities cannot win on raw answer correctness in this
round.

## 1. AI Act systemic-risk threshold

Baseline answer: **not an irrevocable automatic status shortcut.**

Article 51(2) creates the high-impact-capability presumption above 10^25 FLOPs.
Article 52 then matters: notification is required and the provider may make an
exceptional, substantiated case against systemic-risk classification.

The trap was caught by reading the adjacent procedure provision.

## 2. EASA / Airbus ALS

Baseline answer: **Airbus supplies technical content; EASA supplies the public
mandatory legal instruction through the AD.**

EASA's own FAQ is unusually explicit that a manufacturer's use of “mandatory”
does not itself create the same legal status.

The trap was caught by separating document authorship from legal authority.

## 3. Schrems II

Baseline answer: **Privacy Shield invalid; SCC Decision not invalidated.**

The Court separately states that examination of Decision 2010/87 disclosed
nothing affecting its validity, while requiring protection to work in practice
and transfers to be suspended/prohibited where it cannot.

The trap was caught by reading the operative holdings separately.

## 4. Common charger control

Baseline answer: **no laptop requirement on 1 January 2025.**

Laptop application begins 28 April 2026.

This was intentionally easy.

If Needle later claims special value here merely because Temporal can encode the
date, the gate should penalise it.

## 5. Dutch AI Act recital 19

Baseline answer: the 19 December 2025 corrigendum itself documents the earlier
Dutch wording:

> Onlineruimten vallen onder het toepassingsgebied...

and replaces it with:

> Onlineruimten vallen niet onder het toepassingsgebied...

The historical source-state question therefore cannot safely be answered from a
current representation alone.

This was the most manually demanding baseline case, but it was still solvable
without Needle software.
