# Issue #409 — competitor-presence correction

Date: 2026-09-26

## Problem

During the lean-team Candidate-B search, the project began using this implicit rule:

> if an accessible product already offers the capability, the vertical is no longer interesting.

That rule is too strong.

It repeats an older Needle failure mode in a new form: confusing **incumbent existence** with
**absence of residual product value**.

#282 already repaired the analogous scientific rule:

> **Existing is not useless. Different is not valuable. Novel is not demanded.**

The same symmetry applies to product discovery.

## Corrected rule

Competitor presence has three effects:

1. **kills novelty claims** — Needle cannot claim to have invented the job/capability;
2. **strengthens the baseline** — Candidate B must compare against the best accessible product,
   not a spreadsheet strawman;
3. **can support job reality** — paid/self-serve products are evidence that somebody values the
   job enough to productise it.

Competitor presence does **not** by itself answer whether Needle could be:

- cheaper to deploy;
- narrower and simpler;
- more source-transparent;
- more historically precise;
- safer at a difficult cross-owner boundary;
- easier for a small team to self-serve;
- better suited to one regulated niche.

Those require a measurable comparison.

## New vertical retention rule

A vertical is no longer rejected merely because an accessible competitor exists.

Instead classify:

- `OCCUPIED_BUT_TESTABLE` — job is real and competitors exist; at least one measurable Needle
  residual can be tested;
- `PRODUCT_ACCESS_REQUIRED` — public contracts are too close/unclear to discriminate honestly;
- `NO_MEASURABLE_RESIDUAL` — after full incumbent credit, Needle cannot state a concrete testable
  improvement without inventing one;
- `NOT_YET_EVENTFUL` — plausible job, but the operative event needed for a clean internal test has
  not happened yet;
- `NOT_TARGET_SEGMENT` — evidence does not support the intended lean-team user.

`NO_MEASURABLE_RESIDUAL`, not competitor existence, is the product-discovery kill state.

## Consequence for earlier #409 corrections

### CBAM

The retired spreadsheet-vs-Needle replay remains correctly retired.

Accessible CBAM products invalidate that comparator.

But CBAM is reclassified from an implied dead end to:

> **PRODUCT_ACCESS_REQUIRED / OCCUPIED MARKET**

A future frozen workload could compare Needle against one or more actual CBAM point products if
access is available.

### MDR

The retired spreadsheet-vs-Needle replay remains correctly retired.

V5 Ultimate / BSI / other tools strengthen the baseline substantially.

MDR becomes:

> **PRODUCT_ACCESS_REQUIRED / HIGH-PAIN OCCUPIED MARKET**

not 'no value'.

### IVDR

Same logic:

> **PRODUCT_ACCESS_REQUIRED / HIGH-PAIN OCCUPIED MARKET**

### CPR / GAR

Continue product/value comparison rather than searching for an empty market.

## Product-discovery principle

> **Needle does not need a monopoly on the capability. It needs a user-relevant reason to be
> chosen over the strongest realistic alternative.**

This principle does not weaken the scientific baseline rule.

It makes product discovery more realistic.
