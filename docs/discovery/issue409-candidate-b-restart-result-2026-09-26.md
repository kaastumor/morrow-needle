# Issue #409 — Candidate B lean-team restart result

Date: 2026-09-26

## Result

# **CANDIDATE_B_REVISE — LEAN ACCESS GAP -> HARMONISED-STANDARD OPERATIVE-STATUS WEDGE**

The sponsor's restart was productive, but the original lean-team hypothesis was too coarse.

## 1. What the research rejected

Rejected:

> **smaller/lean teams are meaningfully separated from maintained regulatory capability simply
> because enterprise suites are expensive/complex.**

Public research found substantial accessible alternatives:

- CBAM point tools aimed at small importers;
- MDR/IVDR self-serve/free-trial platforms;
- BSI Compliance Navigator explicitly marketed to SMEs;
- general product-compliance tools such as ComplyMatrix;
- small-team regulatory-intelligence plans such as Foresight Essential;
- CE/product-compliance tools with standards monitoring;
- emerging dedicated standards-watch products.

Therefore:

> **lean team != spreadsheet by default.**

Any future Candidate-B comparison must include accessible point products where they exist.

## 2. What the research did not reject

Not rejected:

- smaller organisations face meaningful regulatory/compliance burden;
- EU product-law conclusions can depend on independently moving official state;
- a narrower, simpler product can still compete in an occupied category;
- old Needle legal-state machinery may support a small decision-ready surface;
- competitor existence can support job reality while killing novelty claims.

## 3. Method repair

Canonical product-discovery rule:

> **Competitor presence kills novelty claims, not product value by itself.**

A Candidate is rejected only when, after full incumbent credit, Needle cannot name a concrete
measurable reason a user might choose it.

Durable owner:

> `docs/discovery/issue409-competitor-presence-correction-2026-09-26.md`

## 4. Vertical findings

### MDR / IVDR

Real high-burden SME-heavy jobs, but accessible products publicly claim much of the proposed
condition/document/standards workflow.

Disposition:

> **PRODUCT_ACCESS_REQUIRED — RETAIN HIGH-VALUE VERTICALS**

Do not infer product gaps from public omissions.

### CPR-2024

Strong forward lean-team candidate.

Commission guidance says CPR-2011 and CPR-2024 will coexist, with product families migrating
individually when new performance harmonised standards are cited/made mandatory.

First major migrations are expected from 2027, so the strongest empirical event is still ahead.

Disposition:

> **NOT_YET_EVENTFUL — RETAIN STRONG FORWARD VERTICAL**

### GAR / harmonised-standard status

Immediate current event exists:

> Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1750 formally decided not to publish the OJ
> references to EN 497:2022 and EN 17476:2021/A1:2022.

Official free monitoring is strong, and commercial standards/compliance tools exist.

The narrow residual is therefore not alerts.

It is:

> **decision-ready, historically queryable legal status of a standard's OJ reference and its
> presumption-of-conformity consequence.**

Disposition:

> **OCCUPIED_BUT_TESTABLE — BEST IMMEDIATE INTERNAL WEDGE**

## 5. Historical Needle capital successfully re-used

No new ontology was needed.

The existing:

> `authoritative-dynamic-set-v0.1`

already owns exactly this causal shape:

> legal effect conditioned on authoritative membership/status outside the governing rule's own
> text.

#409 added two new product-oriented regressions without changing the canonical schema:

- GAR EN 497:2022 — formal decision not to publish the OJ reference;
- LVD EN 60335-2-60:2003 — current citation with a legally fixed future withdrawal.

This extends the old proof set beyond:

- Toy Safety — cited reference becomes restricted;
- REACH — agency-list member is added.

## 6. Disposable Candidate-B core

Human-facing core:

> **Harmonised Standard Status Card v0.1**

Durable owner:

> `docs/mvp/candidate-b-harmonised-standard-status-core-v0.1.md`

Disposable renderer:

> `scripts/render_harmonised_standard_status.py`

It answers, as of date T:

- OJ-reference state;
- presumption consequence;
- latest owning event;
- scope/restriction;
- next already-known future transition;
- official evidence;
- forbidden inferences.

Important example:

> EN 497:2022 is `NOT_CITED` both before and after 24 July 2026, but after that date the reason is
> materially stronger: there is a binding Commission decision **not to publish** its reference.

Important future-state example:

> EN 60335-2-60:2003 remains cited on 26 September 2026, while a binding withdrawal is already
> scheduled for 18 January 2027.

These are exactly the historical/current/future distinctions the original Needle architecture was
built to preserve.

## 7. What this proves

Supported:

- Candidate B can reuse old Needle capital instead of rebuilding Full Needle;
- the old dynamic-set model generalises to new 2025/2026 official standards events;
- a small deterministic status surface can preserve current/historical/future legal state;
- no LLM is required for the final status projection once the authoritative transition is encoded.

Not supported:

- that competitors fail these cases;
- that the status card is commercially differentiated;
- that users prefer this output;
- that the cost of maintaining official state is low enough for a viable product;
- that GAR is the largest market;
- that Candidate B is superior to Candidate A/C.

## 8. Strongest red team

The Commission already supplies harmonised-standard lists, formal-objection pages, OJ decisions and
RSS feeds.

Commercial tools such as Certivo, Foresight, ComplyMatrix and Grecta publicly claim standards
monitoring/product relevance/evidence capabilities.

Therefore a single known-standard card may merely repackage an answer a competent user can obtain
quickly.

The product hypothesis only becomes interesting at:

- a portfolio of standards;
- historical/future status;
- restrictions/non-publication/withdrawal semantics;
- product-specific relevance;
- low-friction self-service;
- or a measurable cost/clarity advantage against accessible products.

## 9. Next evidence route

The next useful Candidate-B gate is **not partner outreach**.

Prepare and run a frozen **product-capability challenge** against accessible/self-serve/free-trial
standards/compliance products where possible.

The challenge should use several real formal-objection/status cases and ask the same narrow
questions:

- current OJ-reference state;
- scope of presumption;
- effective date;
- scheduled future transition;
- historical as-of answer;
- official evidence.

This can establish product-capability overlap/differences without claiming user value.

Only after that should the project decide whether Candidate B deserves a broader disposable MVP or
needs actual practitioner evidence.

## Final disposition

# **CANDIDATE_B_REVISE**

Revised hypothesis:

> **A small, evidence-first EU product-compliance state surface may create value for lean
> manufacturers by turning official OJ/reference transitions into decision-ready, historically
> queryable product state. Existing products are the comparator, not a disqualifier.**

Candidate A remains paused but intact.

Candidate C remains live.

CPR remains the strongest forward reuse path if/when real CPR-2024 product-family migrations begin.
