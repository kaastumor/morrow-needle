## Result

# **IMPLEMENTATION_QA_RESIDUAL_NULL — MATTER_SPECIFIC_PREVALENCE_UNRESOLVED**

A frozen 16-question sample from official EU implementation-Q&A / FAQ channels produced:

- **12/16 — TEXT_SUFFICIENT**
- **4/16 — ORDINARY_CROSS_INSTRUMENT**
- **0/16 — CHANGE_HISTORY_SUFFICIENT**
- **0/16 — INDEPENDENT_OPERATIVE_STATE**
- **0/16 — INDETERMINATE**

This is a genuine negative result for a broad prevalence thesis.

Within this externally sourced implementation-Q&A frame, the difficult questions were
predominantly difficult because of interpretation, definitions, interacting provisions or
ordinary Level-1/Level-2 legal research. None required reconstructing the stronger residual
identified in Phase 2: a separately moving operative state whose omission would reverse or
materially distort the answer and which could not be reduced to ordinary text/amendment
research.

The Phase 2 boundary therefore survives as an **existence claim**, but Phase 3A supplies no
support that the residual is common in generic implementation questions.

- European Banking Authority DORA Q&A;
- ESMA MiCA Q&A;

- European Commission MDR Annex XVI transitional Q&A.

Questions were frozen in external display/document order before detailed answer

This still is **not a population-random sample of EU-law work**. It is a sample of official
implementation questions.

## Source-contract cautions

### EBA Q&A

The EBA expressly warns that its Q&As refer to provisions in force on the date of
publication and are not systematically reviewed when legislation is later amended.

That limitation is important evidence about Q&A reuse, but it is **not counted as an
independent operative-state residual in the underlying legal question**.

A stale answer may be a source-view problem; the frozen task here is the legal question the
Q&A was answering at publication.

Official source:
https://www.eba.europa.eu/single-rule-book-qa/all

### ESMA Q&A

ESMA's MiCA Q&A batch is an external implementation-question source. The answers clarify the
application of MiCA; they do not create a separate moving legal state merely because a
regulator issued interpretive guidance.

Official batch:
https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/new-qas-available-26

### EUDR FAQ

The Commission describes the FAQ as Commission-service guidance rather than binding law.

That means the legal answer still has to be checked against the Regulation and authoritative
legal owners. But a non-binding explanatory layer is not itself a Needle residual.

Official source:
https://environment.ec.europa.eu/publications/faq-eudr-implementation_en

### MDR Annex XVI Q&A

The Annex XVI questions are valuable precisely because they expose detailed transition
rules. Yet the first four frozen questions ask for the general dates/rules themselves, not
whether a particular device currently satisfies a certificate/cohort/application/notified-
body condition.

That distinction explains why Phase 2 could find a real MDR matter-state residual while
Phase 3A's generic MDR Q&A does not.

Official guidance index:
https://health.ec.europa.eu/medical-devices-sector/new-regulations/guidance-mdcg-endorsed-documents-and-other-guidance_en

## Why this is a real null

The result is not:

> the questions were easy.

Several are not.

Nor is the result:

> one legal text always suffices.

Four require ordinary cross-instrument research.

The result is narrower:

> **none of the 16 externally sourced generic implementation questions required the
> independently moving operative-state residual defined after Phase 2.**

That directly reduces confidence in any broad claim that EU legal-state reconstruction is a
common requirement across implementation questions.

The project must not replace these questions with more favorable ones and call the combined
exercise representative.

---

## What the null does not establish

The sample frame itself has a structural limitation.

Official regulator/Commission Q&A systems naturally select for questions that can be
answered as **general interpretations**:

- what does a term mean?
- which provision applies?
- how do two rules interact?
- what is the transition rule?
- what must an operator generally do?

They are less likely to publish the full factual record needed for questions such as:

- has this entity actually been designated/identified?
- did this particular certificate remain valid through the transition?
- which Member-State option was exercised for this undertaking?
- what authoritative price/list/status applied to this transaction at T?
- did this particular product family migrate into the new regime?
- was the relevant standard actually cited in the OJ at the operative date?

Those are exactly the kinds of matter-specific questions in which Phase 2 found the stronger
residual.

Therefore Phase 3A cannot support:

> **independent-state prevalence is zero across real EU-law work.**

The proper interpretation is:

> **generic official implementation-Q&A does not reproduce the Phase 2 residual; prevalence
> in matter-specific work remains unresolved.**

---

## Needle mapping

No post-hoc Needle class mapping is needed for the primary result because there are **zero
independent-state residuals**.

That is preferable to forcing ordinary Q&A interpretation into existing classes.

The EBA temporal disclaimer could resemble `SOURCE_VIEW_TEMPORAL_DIVERGENCE` if a stale Q&A
were actually relied upon after its governing law changed, but that is a separate failure
scenario and was not the frozen question.

---

## Consequence for the EU research programme

Phase 3A weakens the broadest EU thesis.

Current evidence now says:

1. **Phase 2:** independently moving operative states demonstrably exist across several
   contemporary EU regimes;
2. **Phase 3A:** they do **not** appear in a 16-question sample of generic official
   implementation Q&A;
3. therefore the key unresolved boundary is **matter-specific legal work**, not generic
   implementation interpretation.

The project should not immediately select a matter-specific sample because that could become
post-null rescue.

A direction review is required first.

It should compare:

- **PARK** — accept that the residual is real but too sparse / matter-specific to justify
  further research;
- **DISCOVER SOURCE FRAME** — identify a mechanically selectable corpus of real
  matter-specific EU-law problems before running any new prevalence test;
- **COMMERCIAL CONTRACT REVIEW** — inspect whether strong regulatory-intelligence products
  already represent the residual state sufficiently;
- **SWITCH FRONT** — return current WIP to the preserved legal-AI evaluation-science /
  evaluation-contract research programme.

No continuation is justified merely because a more favorable matter-specific example can be
imagined.
