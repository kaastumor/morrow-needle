# Candidate A — external MVP value-test contract v0.1

Status: **PREPARED ONLY — OUTREACH NOT AUTHORISED**

## 1. Claim under test

> **DELIVERY:** For a real legal-evaluation maintenance event, a Needle-derived Maintenance
> Delta workflow can reach the maintainer's accepted legal/evaluation quality with materially
> lower total qualified-expert burden, rework or turnaround than the maintainer's actual
> current process.

This is not a claim of:

- better legal reasoning;
- better benchmark methodology;
- better legal oracles by default;
- corpus-assisted correctness;
- proprietary legal knowledge.

## 2. External owner

The pilot owner must be someone who actually maintains a legal evaluation estate, for example:

- independent legal benchmark/evaluation operator;
- legal-tech vendor QA/evaluation team;
- legal department with a maintained private legal-AI evaluation set.

The owner supplies the job and acceptance standard.

Needle does not invent the workload after seeing likely difficulty.

## 3. Eligible maintenance event

Preferred:

- law/source change;
- stale/wrong score-bearing legal proposition;
- criterion activation/coverage/consistency repair;
- accepted-answer boundary revision;
- task/rubric semantic revision;
- real legal-AI failure/postmortem that must become a regression.

The event must affect a real maintained evaluation item.

A synthetic Needle-authored defect is not sufficient for the first value test.

## 4. Incumbent comparator

The comparator is:

> **the maintainer's actual current maintenance workflow**

not a simplified control created for the experiment.

Record enough to expose hidden work:

- qualified legal time;
- non-lawyer evaluation/engineering time;
- model/tool use where material;
- source reopening;
- review/adjudication;
- task/rubric edits;
- historical-result handling;
- rework rounds;
- elapsed turnaround.

If the maintainer already uses an artifact equivalent to the Maintenance Delta Card, give it
full credit.

That can falsify Candidate A quickly.

## 5. Needle-assisted intervention

The human-facing intervention is only:

> **Maintenance Delta Card**

It records:

1. subject/baseline;
2. trigger;
3. changed score-bearing state;
4. evidence/time owner where needed;
5. affected contract/risk;
6. repair status;
7. prior-result comparability/action;
8. adjudication state.

Morrow may maintain the structured JSON record internally.

The external user should not be asked to operate Needle taxonomy, schemas or a new UI.

## 6. Work division

Morrow may:

- inspect the supplied task/contract and permitted sources;
- draft the delta record/card;
- identify likely affected criteria/results;
- propose a repair;
- identify unresolved questions.

The qualified external owner/reviewer must:

- own the legal/evaluation acceptance decision;
- correct any legal or contract errors;
- decide whether the repair is accepted;
- decide result comparability / rejudge / rerun action where judgment is needed.

All qualified correction/adjudication time counts against the Needle-assisted burden.

## 7. Pilot designs

### Preferred — two real matched maintenance events

The owner supplies two events considered reasonably comparable **before** execution.

- one uses the actual incumbent workflow;
- one uses the Needle-assisted workflow;
- assignment/order is frozen before work begins;
- the owner records limitations in matching.

This is still a tiny MVP pilot, not population science.

### Acceptable weaker design — one real event + recent incumbent baseline

If only one real event is available:

- predeclare a recent comparable incumbent maintenance baseline from owner records;
- run the Needle-assisted event prospectively;
- compare burden cautiously;
- do not present the result as a controlled causal estimate.

A retrospective "I think this usually takes X" estimate is weak evidence and must be labelled
as such.

## 8. Acceptance floor

Before execution, the owner defines:

- minimum accepted legal/evaluation quality;
- what expert-burden reduction would be **material enough to change behavior**;
- acceptable correction/rework;
- whether turnaround matters;
- what would make the owner use the workflow again.

Needle does not set the success threshold after seeing the result.

## 9. Primary value measures

Required:

- final accepted / rejected;
- total qualified-expert minutes/hours;
- number and severity of corrections;
- rework rounds;
- unresolved disputes;
- elapsed turnaround;
- material non-lawyer/model/tool cost.

Secondary:

- source reopenings;
- number of result-impact decisions;
- historical outputs successfully re-judged rather than rerun;
- future reopening avoided on the second use.

Do not count as value:

- number of Needle fields;
- number of class matches;
- document length;
- metadata volume;
- prettier structure.

## 10. Strong behavior signal

One accepted maintenance event is not enough.

The strongest first-MVP signal is:

> **the owner chooses or commits a second real maintenance event to the workflow after seeing
> the first result and its burden.**

This can also be a Candidate-C-triggered postmortem event.

## 11. Kill rules

Stop building Candidate A if any of these hold:

- accepted quality is worse;
- the owner's predeclared material-burden threshold is not met;
- apparent savings are shifted into later legal review/adjudication;
- the incumbent already supplies an equivalent delta artifact at equal or lower burden;
- the card adds process overhead without changing maintenance decisions;
- the owner would not reuse it after an accepted first result.

A Candidate-A null does **not** falsify Candidate B or C.

## 12. Candidate C relationship

A real postmortem may be the most natural first trigger.

If so:

> Candidate C is the **entry path** and the same maintenance-delta core is the intervention.

Do not relabel Candidate C as proven or rejected based on Candidate A's allocation alone.

## 13. Candidate B boundary

EU operative-state reconstruction remains a separate secondary candidate.

If an evaluation maintenance event happens to depend on EU cross-owner state, reference that
evidence owner in the card.

Do not turn the pilot into an EU legal-state product test.

## 14. Governance

No external contact is authorised by this document.

The sponsor must separately authorise outreach.

If authorised later:

- seek one pilot owner;
- do not run mass outreach;
- do not pitch a finished product;
- do not claim Needle superiority;
- share the value question and test contract, not the project's full historical machinery.
