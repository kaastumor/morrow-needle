## Evaluation-integrity gate after red team

The gate is retained as ordinary evaluation hygiene and compressed into five working groups:

1. **contract adequacy** — requested central work must matter, valid alternatives must not be
   falsely rejected, conditions must be gradable and shared facts must reconcile;
2. **evidence and temporal scope** — score-bearing propositions need the right evidence owner
   and governing time/event;
3. **execution validity** — distinguish evaluator infrastructure failure from failure of a
   capability deliberately included in the tested system;
4. **versioned audit record** — pin effective task state and preserve the revealed execution
   record;
5. **decision-sensitive uncertainty** — separate answer/system variance from judge variance
   when it could change the decision.

Repeated judging measures judge variability; it does not prove correctness or eliminate
shared bias.

A legitimate benchmark revision is not itself a defect. The concern is invalid or
undisclosed comparison across changed score-bearing contracts.

## External DELIVERY hypothesis

The only commercially eligible claim remains:

> **DELIVERY:** Can a Needle-derived workflow produce an already acceptable legal
> evaluation/oracle package with materially lower total qualified-expert effort, rework,
> maintenance burden or turnaround than the evaluator's actual alternative?

This is not a METHOD claim.

A credible external test must be defined by an evaluator rather than Needle and must include:

- an independently supplied workload;
- the evaluator's actual alternative/workflow;
- a qualified independent acceptance decision;
- predeclared quality threshold;
- **all** qualified-expert time, including briefing, checking, correction and adjudication;
- other labour/model/tool cost sufficient to prevent hidden cost shifting;
- rework rounds, unresolved disputes and turnaround.

Equal or better quality must be externally accepted. Document length, checklist completion,
class matches or metadata volume are not substitutes.

Public evidence of large evaluation effort or scarce expert input establishes production
difficulty. It does not establish a purchasable Needle-shaped gap.
