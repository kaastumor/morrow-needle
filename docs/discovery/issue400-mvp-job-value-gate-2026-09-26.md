---

## 1. Selection rule

The first MVP candidate must have:

- a real user/job owner;
- independent evidence that the job recurs or matters;
- a strong incumbent workflow we can compare against honestly;
- a bounded value hypothesis;
- a disposable first intervention;
- a measurable external acceptance contract;
- a plausible behavior-change/repeat-use signal;
- no conflict with the project's negative evidence.

The comparison is deliberately **not** collapsed into a score.

A candidate can lose first priority while retaining real potential.

# Candidate A — legal evaluation/oracle maintenance + delivery

## Status

# **LEAD_MVP_CANDIDATE**

## Job reality

This is the strongest externally evidenced job of the three.

Legal Benchmarks currently operates:

- model and application leaderboards;
- lawyer-authored task criteria;
- independent legal-AI application evaluation;
- qualified-lawyer escalation for pass-changing judge disagreement;
- quarterly application certification / retesting;
- private vendor diagnostics.

Its public framework is built from legal teams' real procurement/evaluation practice, and
the benchmark estate is maintained rather than being a one-off research artifact.

Harvey LAB separately demonstrates that legal-AI vendors maintain complex agentic legal
evaluation tasks and all-pass rubric estates.

Prior #375 evidence also identified:

- paid independent legal-AI evaluation;
- recurring retesting/certification;
- documented dependence on scarce qualified legal input;
- historically large fully-manual scoring burdens in public legal-AI evaluation work.

Therefore the job itself is no longer speculative.

Public sources:

- https://www.legalbenchmarks.ai/methodology
- https://www.legalbenchmarks.ai/research/evaluation-framework
- https://www.harvey.ai/blog/introducing-harveys-legal-agent-benchmark
- https://www.harvey.ai/blog/legal-agent-benchmark-initial-results

## Pain evidence

The burden is not simply "running an eval".

The costly parts include:

- authoring/maintaining legally correct reference answers and criteria;
- checking source/effect time;
- handling acceptable alternatives;
- adjudicating judge disagreement;
- updating tasks after legal or benchmark-contract change;
- preserving version/comparison integrity.

#375 found enough public evidence to classify independent legal-AI evaluation as a real
repeated job, but **not** enough to show Needle lowers that burden.

That distinction remains binding.

## Strongest incumbent

The incumbent is already strong:

> expert-authored task + reference answer + criteria + automated judging + qualified human
> escalation + version/retest discipline.

Needle does not get credit for merely reproducing this.

## Needle leverage

Potential leverage is narrow and compatible with the surviving project asset:

- source/version capture;
- explicit governing-time owner;
- valid-alternative policy;
- fatal/non-compensatory conditions;
- evidence closure;
- change/revision rationale;
- preserved uncertainty/adjudication state;
- regression-ready known-failure boundaries.

The **26 class labels are not the MVP**.

The value hypothesis is delivery efficiency/maintainability at an accepted quality floor.

## Smallest disposable MVP

Not software.

The first MVP is a **manual / model-assisted legal evaluation maintenance packet** for one
external evaluator-owned item.

Candidate packet:

1. task / intended decision;
2. authoritative source set + governing event/time;
3. accepted legal proposition / reference answer;
4. observable criteria;
5. valid alternatives;
6. fatal / non-compensatory failures;
7. evidence map;
8. version/revision state;
9. known uncertainty / adjudication notes;
10. compact change record suitable for future retest.

Anything the evaluator does not need is removed.

## Distinct value hypothesis

> For a real legal-evaluation item, the Needle-derived maintenance/delivery workflow can
> reach the evaluator's existing acceptance standard with materially lower **qualified
> expert burden and rework** than the evaluator's actual current workflow.

This is **DELIVERY**, not METHOD or correctness superiority.

## Measurement contract

The external evaluator must define **before execution**:

- workload;
- actual incumbent workflow;
- acceptance threshold;
- what expert time is material enough to matter.

Measure at minimum:

- total qualified-lawyer minutes/hours;
- legal corrections by severity;
- rework rounds;
- unresolved disagreement;
- elapsed turnaround;
- non-lawyer/model/tool cost sufficient to expose cost shifting;
- final acceptance / rejection.

Do not count packet length, metadata volume or class matches as value.

## Repeat-use / behavior signal

A positive first result is not enough.

The stronger MVP signal is:

> after seeing one accepted result and its burden, the evaluator chooses to use the workflow
> on a second item or explicitly commits another real item to it.

Survey enthusiasm alone is weaker evidence.

## Kill rule

Stop first-MVP investment in this job if:

- accepted quality is worse;
- total qualified burden is not materially lower by the evaluator's predeclared threshold;
- savings come from shifting hidden work to later adjudication;
- the user would not reuse the workflow;
- the incumbent already produces the same artifact at equal/lower burden.

Do not rescue a null as "better methodology".

## External dependency

Hard external gate:

- evaluator-owned workload;
- actual incumbent process;
- qualified acceptance owner.

Public research cannot substitute for this.

# Candidate C — known failure/postmortem -> reusable regression/oracle

## Status

# **SECONDARY_CANDIDATE**

This is a credible candidate with particularly strong alignment to Needle's surviving asset.

It is not selected first because the **generic mechanics are already heavily productised**,
and the remaining legal-specific value needs a real incident owner.

## Job reality

The generic AI-engineering job is unquestionably real.

Braintrust explicitly markets a workflow in which production failures are:

- captured from traces;
- labeled;
- promoted into regression datasets;
- given scorers;
- run in CI/live evaluation.

Arize Phoenix similarly connects:

- production traces;
- datasets;
- annotations;
- evaluations;
- experiments;
- regression checking.

Therefore Needle should **not** build generic failure-to-test plumbing.

Public sources:

- https://www.braintrust.dev/learn/ai-testing/v0
- https://customers-page-2.preview.braintrust.dev/articles/turn-llm-production-failures-into-regression-tests
- https://arize.com/docs/phoenix/quickstart
- https://arize.com/docs/phoenix/evaluation/llm-evals/evaluator-traces

## Legal-specific residual

The potential Needle contribution is upstream of generic eval infrastructure:

> turn a consequential **legal** failure into a legally valid regression oracle that preserves
> source authority, legal time, accepted alternatives, opposite-error boundaries and
> revision state.

A generic eval platform can store/run the test once that oracle exists.

Needle would have to improve the **oracle conversion**, not compete with Braintrust/Phoenix
on datasets, tracing or CI.

## Positive internal evidence

- Needle's surviving identity is known-failure reference/regression memory;
- #339/#353 observed bounded usefulness in failure-analysis packets;
- the corpus contains many concrete examples of source/time/status traps.

## Counterevidence

- #349 showed a strong ordinary source-linked postmortem can already be sufficient;
- #362 weakens broad claims of residual artifact advantage;
- generic eval platforms already solve the operational regression loop.

Therefore the incremental job is likely concentrated in **hard legal incidents**, not every
failure.

## Smallest disposable MVP

If/when a real incident owner appears:

> take one real legal-AI production failure and produce a minimal accepted regression-oracle
> artifact that plugs into the owner's existing eval platform.

No new test runner, dashboard or trace store.

## Measurement

Compare against the team's strongest ordinary postmortem-to-test process on:

- qualified time to accepted regression;
- number/severity of oracle corrections;
- reopenings needed at a later retest;
- whether the regression catches the intended failure without rejecting valid alternatives;
- repeat use on a second incident.

## Promotion trigger

Promote C toward lead status if an external legal-AI team provides:

- a real consequential failure;
- an existing trace/postmortem;
- its current regression process;
- willingness to compare oracle-conversion burden.

It may become an especially good **wedge into Candidate A**, because a discovered failure can
create a concrete maintenance/oracle task.

# Candidate B — EU operative-state reconstruction across authoritative owners

## Status

# **SECONDARY_CANDIDATE**

This remains a genuine potential vertical.

It is not selected first because the exact residual user burden is less well established and
the incumbent market is stronger than the early Needle framing assumed.

## Job reality

The underlying work is real:

- legal/regulatory teams must determine applicability;
- regulatory change systems map changes to obligations/controls;
- complex sectors use specialised state systems such as EUDAMED;
- historical/current legal research requires version/effect tracking.

#377/#395 showed that some questions require composition across:

- legislation;
- national options/law;
- administrative state;
- standards/citation state;
- certificate/device state;
- dynamic authoritative inputs.

That phenomenon remains real.

## Incumbent correction

The incumbent is much stronger than "search EUR-Lex manually".

Current public products claim substantial parts of the workflow:

- CUBE: regulatory change lifecycle, applicability, mapped obligations, ownership and audit
  trail;
- Bloomberg Regology: Smart Law Library, customer-specific "legal DNA", causal citations,
  change/applicability management and compliance workflow;
- Thomson Reuters Regulatory Intelligence: targeted regulatory intelligence;
- vLex: in-force state, historical versions and amendment tracking;
- official EU systems themselves increasingly own domain state directly.

Public sources:

- https://www.cube.global/products/regplatform/regulatory-change-management
- https://regology.com/regulatory-change-agent
- https://regology.com/platform
- https://legal.thomsonreuters.com/en/insights/articles/regulatory-intelligence-not-all-content-adds-value
- https://knowledge.vlex.com/en/document-types/legislation/versions-and-amendments

## Positive Needle evidence

The surviving residual is coherent:

> a lawyer may still have to compose several authoritative owners and legal conditions into
> one operative conclusion for actor/product/matter/time.

That is narrower than generic regulatory intelligence and could matter greatly in specific
regulated domains.

## Counterevidence

- #377 did not support broad prevalence in generic implementation work;
- the CJEU matter-specific contrast found only one strong residual in twelve cases;
- #395 found **zero confirmed official-model gaps** across the seven residual structures;
- product capabilities beyond public documentation remain unknown.

So there is no basis for saying current systems "leave this out".

## Smallest disposable MVP

A future test should not be an EU-law platform.

It should be:

> one practitioner-owned operative-state question, one incumbent research workflow, and one
> disposable cross-owner evidence/composition artifact.

Example output could be a compact evidence graph/memo that identifies:

- controlling rule;
- current/historical state owners;
- governing time;
- external status/value;
- unresolved inference;
- final operative conclusion.

Only the fields that change practitioner behavior survive.

## Measurement

On a real matter:

- qualified research/review time;
- source reopenings;
- corrections;
- missed owner/state dependencies;
- confidence/acceptance;
- later maintenance burden;
- actual willingness to reuse.

## Promotion trigger

Promote B toward lead status if:

- an EU practitioner/compliance team presents a recurring cross-owner reconstruction job;
- the incumbent workflow shows material reopening/manual-composition burden;
- or a regulatory-intelligence product/user reveals a concrete state-composition gap.

Because the work can be highly consequential, **low population prevalence does not make it
useless**. It changes the likely market shape from broad legal research to a narrower
high-complexity vertical.

# Frozen first MVP external test contract

## Target user

One legal-AI evaluation/oracle owner, preferably:

- independent benchmark/evaluation operator; or
- legal-tech vendor/internal QA team with a maintained legal benchmark/eval estate.

## Target job

One **real legal evaluation item** that needs either:

- creation; or preferably
- maintenance/repair because law, source state, accepted interpretation or the evaluation
  contract changed.

Maintenance is preferred because it exposes version/evidence/rework burden directly.

## Two work products

### Incumbent arm

The evaluator produces/repairs the item using its **actual current workflow**.

### Needle-assisted arm

From the same frozen workload brief/source access, Morrow produces the smallest candidate
maintenance packet.

To avoid pretending Morrow is a qualified lawyer, the Needle-assisted arm's qualified legal
review/correction time is included in full.

The evaluator/reviewer controls final legal acceptance.

## Blinding / contamination

Where feasible:

- the final deliverables are reviewed blind to arm;
- authors do not see the other arm before completion;
- reviewer corrections/disagreements are preserved;
- if same-task contamination makes a fair comparison impossible, use two evaluator-supplied
  matched items and predeclare the limitation.

Do not fabricate scientific precision from a one-item pilot.

The MVP question is behavioral/value-oriented.

## Success contract

Before the pilot, the evaluator defines:

1. minimum accepted legal quality;
2. what qualified-expert time reduction would be material enough to matter;
3. which rework/correction level is acceptable;
4. whether faster turnaround matters;
5. what would make them actually reuse the workflow.

Needle succeeds provisionally only if:

- legal quality is accepted;
- the material burden threshold is met without hidden work transfer;
- and the evaluator chooses or commits to a second real use.

## Kill rule

If accepted quality or burden does not beat the evaluator's threshold:

> **do not build the MVP around Candidate A.**

Return to the retained candidate set.

C and B remain available; they are not converted to failures by A's result.
