## 2. What this prototype actually is

Needle EU now supports one bounded public-understanding journey:

> ordinary medical-device question or exact represented identifier
> -> source-bound evidence/locator
> -> bounded medical overview or MDR/IVDR detail
> -> typed time / relationships / official evidence
> -> return/deep navigation

It also contains:
- one bounded non-medical contrast page for Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/1022;
- the pre-existing narrow known-standard-status tool;
- the retained deeper regime/relationship view.

It is **not** a comprehensive EU-law service, legal-advice engine, applicability classifier,
national-law engine, live monitoring service or multilingual legal-answer system.
