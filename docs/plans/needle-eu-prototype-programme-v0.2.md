# Needle EU — bounded prototype programme

Reconciled 30 September 2026 after the strategic audit and brainstorm review.
This is the existing delivery contract, not a second live queue;
[BACKLOG.md](../../BACKLOG.md) alone owns mode, WIP and next action.

## Product purpose and bounded first job

The [charter](../project-charter.md) owns the public-understanding objective and the
distinction between the supported reference asset and the product hypothesis.

The first delivery job is **ordinary question or known act -> relevant information ->
understandable orientation -> useful depth -> verifiable official evidence**. Question-first
does not require a chatbot. The initial audience hypothesis is an occasional non-specialist
EU-law reader; journalist and company examples supply task perspectives, not separate
products or an automatic professional-compliance commitment.

Keep the accepted restrained editorial presentation, timeline and typed relationship
inspection where useful. Start with purpose, scope/roles, main effect/change, material
date/status and caveat; progressively expose source passages, versions and justified
research paths. Verification remains available in Public mode. No fictional regime-wide
legal status, expiry or impact score; no manufactured surprising connection.

Contextual citizen awareness (#493) is a second, separately evaluated discovery job.
It starts without a named act, dispute or question and allows awareness without immediate
action. It is not included as an implemented v1 promise. Its delivery gate remains;
known-act success is not a value prerequisite for testing it. Exact decision/related-vote
history (#494) remains a separate research comparison with its participant pause intact.

## Outcome and finite finish line

A reader can find covered EU legislation, understand its purpose and relevance, inspect
represented core acts and important dates/relationships, then verify consequential
statements. The maintained public set is explicit and small. Wider adversarial fixtures
do not automatically become maintained public content.

**Prototype v1 is ready for sponsor review** when:

- the bounded public content set, actual language/source/version coverage and evidence
  dates are explicit;
- journeys A-G below work on a built revision, including substantive represented core-act
  detail and an ordinary-question entry; no inert promised controls or disconnected acts;
- material qualifications and official evidence remain accessible at every depth;
- #492's applicable development/regression/held-back results and unresolved significant
  failures are recorded honestly;
- actual browser/reflow/keyboard evidence is recorded for the reviewed revision; blocked
  execution stays an open gate;
- one consequential correction has been rehearsed, with legal review, affected claims,
  repair and rework accounted for;
- the build/update procedure, demonstration, limits and next human comparison are explicit.

Repair demonstrated critical misrepresentation or broken committed journeys. Broader
coverage, another redesign or an arbitrary pass-rate target is not a new readiness gate.
If the bounded contract cannot be completed proportionately, record REVISE/NARROW/PARK
rather than quietly add architecture or keep preparing indefinitely.

Reviewable code, sponsor acceptance, merge and public release remain separate statuses.
Only sponsor review can close the prototype milestone. Sponsor investment authorizes
delivery; it does not demonstrate reader preference, comprehension, comparative value or
commercial demand. #214, the frozen scientific corpus and LVD evaluation,
#327/#366/#377/#392/#395/#407/#411 and Candidate A/C evidence gates remain intact.

## Observable journeys

| Journey | v1 acceptance scenario |
| --- | --- |
| A — Find and enter | Ordinary wording, common names/aliases and exact identifiers lead to a relevant covered result and an explanation of the match. Challenge ambiguous/false premises. Unknown identifiers do not substitute a nearby act. Outside-coverage and missing-resource states are explicit, not proof of no law. Deep links open the represented page. |
| B — Understand | A short public explanation states purpose, affected roles/products, main effects/change and material exclusions. Deeper detail expands the same facts; shortening and mode changes cannot delete a decision-changing qualification. |
| C — Inspect acts | Every represented core act opens a substantive detail view with purpose/scope/dates/selected changes/typed relationships/sources. Supporting external source links are labelled as handoffs, not promised internal detail views. |
| D — Understand time | Source-linked milestones distinguish adoption, publication, entry into force, application, conditional transition, operational trigger and proposal where relevant. No end or effective date is invented; schematic spacing is labelled. |
| E — Understand relationships | Represented nodes/typed links distinguish editorial grouping, replacement, amendment and implementing/delegated relationships in words as well as styling. Proposal is non-law; selection is explicit; navigation edges are not automatic legal-impact dependencies. |
| F — Verify and navigate | Nearby official support, actual source language/version, evidence dates and unresolved points remain visible. Back/deep links work. Preview is not a complete legal answer; consequential context can be inspected. Link activation and successful destination retrieval are reported separately. |
| G — Use reliably | Desktop, 390px, 320px, actual 200% browser page zoom, keyboard/focus, disclosures, invalid/no-result states and missing-script behavior are exercised on the built revision. Unavailable data/runtime is labelled rather than filled or claimed PASS. |

## Search and discovery contract

All official EU languages are a design requirement for one common search foundation,
not a later NL-only translation patch. Separate query language, UI language, actual source
language, expression/version, translation state and user-supplied jurisdiction. Language
does not infer jurisdiction. Other-language evidence is opt-in and labelled; missing packs
and translations stay visible. Report substantive quality by actually tested resources and
queries; a language registry or synthetic check is not all-language comprehension evidence.

Recover and inspect the retained local search/result artifact linked in
[#490's checkpoint](https://github.com/kaastumor/morrow-needle/issues/490#issuecomment-5889536165)
before integrating it. Verify saved identity/checksum, retained engine versus rejected
field-ranker, consumer instructions, source scope and actual browser integration. A reported
local result is not repository/deployment evidence. Keep experimental consumer/native-language
sources distinct from maintained medical coverage; sharing an index must not silently
promote them to reviewed legal explanations. Do not repeat exposed ranker comparisons or
add a semantic model/provider without a newly demonstrated consequential gap.

The first working search may use a declared captured/source set. That is an implementation
boundary, not a redefinition of the sponsor's web-assisted discovery ambition. Preserve
three evidence states when discovery is available:
1. maintained/source-checked Needle explanation;
2. newly found official material not yet fully analysed;
3. reporting/commentary, useful as a lead rather than authority for operative status.

Resolve identity, date/version, jurisdiction and source before consequential claims.
Agent-side web research does not prove an in-product search service. Before adding an
external discovery interface, verify its provider, actual runtime/access, quotas, cost,
source states and unsupported-result behavior in its own bounded slice. No paid API,
general chatbot, EU-wide ingestion or live monitoring is implicit.

Scenario anchors share the same model:

- journalist asks whether rules were relaxed: identify the development, compare only
  supported states, explain whom/when and retain the decisive passage or uncertainty;
- importing/distributing diagnostic products: find relevant scope and changes, identify
  missing role/jurisdiction/time facts where material, avoid a definitive applicability
  or compliance verdict.

## Diverse-law campaign — #492

[#492](https://github.com/kaastumor/morrow-needle/issues/492) owns the case-selection,
exposure and execution contract. Its approved wider test scope supersedes the old
single-contrasting-example limit for adversarial testing, not public maintenance.

First tranche: a compact diversity matrix, first three structurally different real
primary-source-supported development cases, expected outcomes fixed before exercising
the available product path. The planning default is about twelve cases: eight development
cases including two ordinary positive controls, plus four reserved post-repair checks.
This is not a statistical or completeness claim. Do not use reserved cases for tuning;
after exposure they become regressions. Do not relabel known corpus material as fresh.

Exercise Find -> identity/version/time -> summary -> detail/dates/relationships -> evidence
and useful next research path where implemented. Preserve original input, sources,
expectations, exact revision, actual output, failed layer, severity, repair and replay.
Use PASS/FAIL/SAFE_LIMIT/BLOCKED/NOT_TESTED honestly. Safe outside-coverage behavior is not
useful-answer success; blanket abstention on a committed in-coverage task is a failure.
Discovery may proceed before all features exist, with unavailable capabilities untested.
No extra parallel worker, schedule, public catalogue or scientific-corpus growth follows.

## External learning and extension decisions

After finite readiness and the separately required participant authority, compare the
orientation job with competent official navigation or a strong source-linked note under
the same task/scope. Measure consequential understanding/errors, useful completion and
total effort, not only preference or clicks. Frozen historical evaluation criteria remain
unchanged.

Recommend contextual awareness as the next distinct strategic learning question, subject
to #493's explicit allocation/delivery gates. Use a fresh unprimed context, declared
coverage and relevance/material-omission checking against a strong realistic alternative.
A supplied act list does not test discovery; the exposed hand-curated paper overview is
development material. Evaluate awareness, participation and preparation separately.
A failed orientation comparison does not dispose of awareness, nor does a useful overview
prove graphs or historical machinery necessary. Run one bounded slice at a time.

If the realistic baseline is sufficient for a tested job, stop/narrow that claim.
If both public jobs lack consequential advantage, stop public-product expansion rather
than rescue it with another feature. If one works with manageable maintenance, earn one
extension from the observed bottleneck or useful next task:

- exact decision provenance: #494's bounded comparison before general related history;
- currentness burden: smallest claim/provision-aware maintenance mechanism;
- professional incident/workflow: revisit A/C only with their real external owner/input.

At M4, give a short #418 portfolio recommendation, not a new internal replay or automatic
activation. Public-interest usefulness and commercial viability remain different decisions.

## Red-team and architecture controls

| Failure pressure | Control / falsifier |
| --- | --- |
| Architecture searches for a product | Reuse the smallest component required by a committed journey or demonstrated failure; historical capability is not demand |
| A polished map implies completeness or hierarchy | Explicit selection and relationship types; misleading graph simplifies to a typed list |
| Public/Expert creates inconsistent truth | One shared content contract and public caveats/sources; repair divergence before dependent pages |
| Search implies comprehensive or multilingual legal advice | Declared source/quality coverage, opt-in fallback, exact identity and uncertainty; no-law and no-hit remain distinct |
| Too many reader jobs | Complete the first orientation slice; keep contextual discovery, standards practitioners, voting research and evaluators separately evaluated and gated |
| Upkeep scales faster than benefit | Count legal review/rework, rehearse a correction, narrow content before introducing general monitoring |
| Internal tests become self-grading user evidence | Preserve revision-level engineering results; human/comparative outcomes need actual external observations |
| Readiness preparation never ends | Finite acceptance checklist; only demonstrated defects justify repairs; broader ambitions do not silently enlarge v1 |
