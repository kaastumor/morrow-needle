## Warm introduction — short

> I’m testing two ways of getting oriented around an EU directive: a structured legislation page and a strong source-linked note using the same evidence. It takes about 20–25 minutes. I’m interested in whether either makes scope, dates, legal relationships and source verification easier — not in testing your legal knowledge. Would you be willing to try it?

## Professional / compliance reader — short

> I’m running a small formative usability comparison for an EU-legislation navigation prototype. The session is about 20–25 minutes and compares two presentations of the same Low Voltage Directive evidence. I’m specifically looking for where the material saves or wastes navigation/interpretation effort and where it could cause a wrong legal inference. This is not a sales call and I’m not asking for client/confidential information.

## Student / occasional legal reader

> I’m testing whether a structured EU-law explainer actually makes one directive easier to understand than a good source-linked note. The exercise takes roughly 20–25 minutes and is about the material, not your legal knowledge. You’ll answer a few practical questions about scope, dates and how the directive connects to other EU rules.

## Optional scheduling note

> No preparation is needed. Please don’t read up on the Low Voltage Directive beforehand; prior familiarity is fine, but I want to see how the material works as presented.
