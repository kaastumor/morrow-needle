## Public claims

### C1 — framework purpose

Proposed copy:

> The EU medical-device framework sets rules for placing, supplying and putting medical devices and in-vitro diagnostic devices into service in the Union. It is intended to support safe, performant devices, reliable evidence and follow-up after products reach the market.

Basis:
- O1;
- O3;
- MDR/IVDR Article 1;
- EUR-Lex summaries.

Qualification:
- do not imply these are the only objectives/effects;
- “performant” especially reflects medical-device/IVD safety-performance framing, not a single quoted statutory purpose sentence.

### C2 — MDR branch

Proposed:

> MDR — Regulation (EU) 2017/745. Start here for medical devices for human use and accessories. It also reaches specified Annex XVI product groups without an intended medical purpose when the Regulation's conditions apply.

Basis:
- O4 Article 1(1)–(2).

Qualification:
- MDR contains exclusions and boundary rules not exhaustively listed on landing page.

### C3 — IVDR branch

Proposed:

> IVDR — Regulation (EU) 2017/746. Start here for in-vitro diagnostic devices for human use, accessories and performance studies.

Basis:
- O5 Article 1(1).

Qualification:
- exact IVD definition/intended purpose sits in Article 2.

### C4 — laboratory/research-use limit

Proposed:

> General laboratory or research-use products are outside IVDR unless, considering their characteristics, the manufacturer specifically intends them for in-vitro diagnostic examination.

Basis:
- O5 Article 1(3)(a).

This is a material decision-changing limit and stays visible near IVDR scope.

### C5 — economic-operator relevance

Proposed:

> Main regulated supply-chain roles include manufacturers, authorised representatives, importers and distributors. Their responsibilities differ.

Basis:
- O2;
- O4/O5 definitions and Articles 10–14.

Qualification:
- roles are not exhaustive of every legally relevant person/entity;
- unlisted role != exclusion;
- a business may occupy more than one role depending on facts.

### C6 — manufacturer practical effect

Proposed:

> Manufacturers carry the primary device-design/conformity system: compliant design/manufacture, risk/evaluation work and technical documentation.

Basis:
- O4 Article 10;
- O5 Article 10.

Qualification:
- deliberately high-level; not a complete obligation list.

### C7 — importer practical effect

Proposed:

> Importers have their own checks before placing third-country devices on the Union market and have traceability, cooperation and corrective responsibilities.

Basis:
- O2/O3;
- MDR/IVDR Article 13.

Qualification:
- wording does not enumerate all checks;
- role determination requires facts.

### C8 — distributor practical effect

Proposed:

> Distributors must act with due care and perform specified checks before making devices available, with duties around complaints, non-conformity and cooperation.

Basis:
- O2/O3;
- MDR/IVDR Article 14.

Qualification:
- “specified checks” avoids implying every distributor checks every item identically.

### C9 — authorised representative

Proposed:

> A manufacturer outside the Union may appoint an authorised representative under a written mandate for specified regulatory tasks.

Basis:
- MDR/IVDR Article 2 definitions and Article 11.

Qualification:
- not every manufacturer has/needs the same representative situation.

### C10 — affected people vs duty bearers

Proposed:

> Patients, users and healthcare professionals are affected by the safety/performance framework, but that does not make them equivalent to economic operators carrying manufacturer/importer/distributor duties.

Basis:
- O1/O3; MDR/IVDR framework purpose and definitions.

Qualification:
- avoid enumerating every health-professional obligation/special setting here.

### C11 — member-act application dates

Proposed:

> MDR generally applies from 26 May 2021. IVDR generally applies from 26 May 2022.

Basis:
- O1.

Qualification:
- these are member-act dates, not one fictional “regime applies from” date.

### C12 — transition qualifier

Proposed:

> Those general dates do not settle every legacy-device case. Later rules preserve or extend transition routes for specified devices subject to conditions.

Basis:
- O1;
- O7.

Qualification:
- no device-specific deadline is inferred from this sentence.

### C13 — enacted 2024 change

Proposed:

> Regulation (EU) 2024/1860 is enacted law amending both MDR and IVDR around gradual EUDAMED roll-out, information duties concerning interruption/discontinuation of supply and IVDR transitional provisions.

Basis:
- O1;
- official Regulation 2024/1860.

### C14 — 2025 proposal

Proposed:

> COM(2025) 1023 is a Commission proposal, not enacted law at the 27 September 2026 evidence checkpoint.

Basis:
- O1.

Qualification:
- Commission has non-official working texts showing proposed integrations; they have no legal effect.

### C15 — predecessor mapping

Proposed:
- 90/385/EEC -> MDR;
- 93/42/EEC -> MDR;
- 98/79/EC -> IVDR.

Basis:
- O1.

Qualification:
- transition rules can preserve predecessor effects in defined cases.

## Editorial-maintenance rule

A public summary statement may change only when:
1. the source/provision is identified;
2. the material qualification is reviewed;
3. legal-as-of / source-version / editorial-review dates remain distinct where needed;
4. dependent task-answer regressions are updated.

Copy is a projection over evidence, not a second independent owner.
