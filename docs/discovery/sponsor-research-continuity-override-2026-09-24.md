## Direction

# **ACTIVE RESEARCH — WIP=1**

The post-#214 scientific result remains unchanged:

- zero diagnostic rescues;
- no demonstrated latent diagnostic advantage from the index-assisted workflow;
- canonical product/workflow identity remains contracted to a
  regression/reference corpus + minimal evaluation discipline.

What changes is the **operating state**.

The sponsor has explicitly rejected `IDLE BY DESIGN` as a project state.

Morrow // Needle is a sponsored research program. A hypothesis, feature,
experiment or product direction may stop. The research program does not stop
merely because one direction loses.

## New default transition

When active work finishes and no implementation horizon is earned:

1. reconcile the result;
2. perform a brief wide-lens scan across internal evidence and external
   precedent;
3. select exactly one bounded research question for information gain;
4. put that question into WIP;
5. continue research.

The canonical transition is:

> **STOP HYPOTHESIS -> PIVOT TO RESEARCH**

not:

> **STOP HYPOTHESIS -> IDLE PROJECT**

## Guardrails retained

Research continuity does not permit:

- retrying failed experiments until Needle wins;
- choosing harder replacement cases post hoc;
- product/feature accumulation;
- taxonomy growth without strong incumbent-concept first refusal;
- infrastructure for hypothetical scale;
- expensive CI/monitoring churn;
- issue count or corpus growth as a success metric.

WIP remains 1.

Negative results, rejection and taxonomy subtraction remain successful research
outcomes.

## Cost discipline

GitHub Actions remain intentionally lean.

Prefer:

- source research before code;
- issue comments for intermediate evidence;
- one batched canonical reconciliation after a bounded run/cluster;
- no per-source or per-thought commits.
