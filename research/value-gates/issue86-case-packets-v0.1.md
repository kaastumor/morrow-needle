# Issue #86 — Competing identities value gate: fixed case packets

**Protocol stage:** case packets fixed before baseline answers.

The five questions were selected to pressure different failure modes while
keeping one deliberately boring control.

No Needle contract mapping is recorded in this document.

## Cases

### 1. AI Act GPAI systemic-risk threshold

Question:

> If a GPAI model used more than 10^25 FLOPs for training, is it automatically
> and irrevocably a systemic-risk GPAI model?

Trap: threshold/presumption can be collapsed into final status.

### 2. EASA A380 ALS

Question:

> Does Airbus A380 ALS Part 1 Revision 14 itself create the mandatory obligation
> referenced by EASA AD 2026-0154?

Trap: private technical authorship can be collapsed into public legal authority.

### 3. Schrems II

Question:

> Did Schrems II automatically make every SCC-based EU-US transfer unlawful?

Trap: invalidity of one Commission decision can be projected onto a distinct
transfer mechanism that the Court separately reviewed.

### 4. Common charger — boring control

Question:

> Were laptops already subject to the common-charger USB-C requirements on
> 1 January 2025?

Trap: one application date can be projected across all listed product classes.

This is intentionally expected to be answerable by careful ordinary source
reading. Needle should receive no credit merely for representing the date.

### 5. Dutch AI Act recital 19 before its corrigendum

Question:

> What did the authentic Dutch publication say before 19 December 2025, and may
> today's corrected/consolidated representation answer that historical question
> by itself?

Trap: current corrected truth can overwrite historical source-state truth.

## Evaluation order

For each case:

1. strongest boring baseline;
2. Needle Method review;
3. Needle Core review;
4. Full Needle review.

The baseline stage is committed separately before stages 2–4.

No identity wins by feature count.
