# **REVISE — research rule/governance repair; no corpus compression**

The retrospective found **no fatal primary-source defect** in the frozen sample and no
evidence that the current 79-case / 26-class corpus needs immediate taxonomy
compression.

It did find two process defects worth repairing before further breadth work:

1. the project used the incumbent-doctrine baseline inconsistently as a value/novelty
   gate, especially in #218; and

   work resumed, creating avoidable path dependence and stale WIP (#273).

The repair is methodological and governance-level. No case or class is added, deleted,
renamed or merged in #282.

## Frozen source re-check

### #218 — external-reference version semantics

**Primary proposition: supported.**  
**Audit disposition: REPAIRABLE rationale, decision not reversed here.**

The 2023 Joint Handbook expressly distinguishes dynamic and static references. Regulation
(EU) 2024/1781 supplies version-specific external references, while Decision (EU)
2021/1870 directs use of the latest DID-list version.

Official sources:

- https://www.consilium.europa.eu/media/67390/joint_handbook_en_01-october-2023_clean_def_final.pdf
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1781/oj
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec/2021/1870/oj/eng

The defect is not source accuracy. The result says, in substance, that mature reference
doctrine is sufficient and therefore a second Needle class is unnecessary. Later
admissions correctly say the opposite general proposition: mature doctrine may fully own
the **law** while a bounded representation/regression failure can still be worth
preserving.

Therefore #218's REJECT is retained as historical evidence, but its reasoning is not a
valid general rejection rule. If the same candidate ever returns, it must be judged on
residual regression/reference value and lossless decomposition, not novelty or doctrinal
familiarity.

### #245 — scoped third-country recognition

**Primary proposition: supported.**  
**Audit disposition: TOLERABLE / strong REJECT.**

GDPR Article 45 permits adequacy decisions for a third country, territory or specified
sector. The EU-US DPF then adds a dynamic organisation-list condition for the US
framework.

Official sources:

- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec_impl/2023/1795/oj

Unlike #218's wording, #245 gives a stronger rejection argument:

> decision-specific scope is ordinary scope analysis, while dynamic qualifying-list
> status is already owned by `DYNAMIC_REFERENCE_STATUS`.

The candidate decomposes without a residual causal owner. That is the preferred REJECT
standard.

### #220 / #225 — machine compliance artifact

**Primary propositions: supported.**  
**Audit disposition: TOLERABLE; current class survives.**

Regulation (EU) 2024/3110 makes Commission software part of the environmental
performance calculation and creates a publication -> voluntary-use -> mandatory-use
transition. Implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/699 makes the Commission WEEE tool an
integral required calculation tool. Regulation (EU) 2026/1030 gives calculation-tool
identity/certification consequences.

Official sources:

- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/3110/oj
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2017/699/oj
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2026/1030/oj

The original #219/#220 claim that the software examples were highly orthogonal was
somewhat stronger than the evidence: they are different regulatory domains but still all
computation-tool mechanisms.

That weakness was materially cured by #225. Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1778
requires versioned authoritative machine-readable semantic profiles for Digital Product
Passport data, extending the class beyond executable code.

Source:

- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2026/1778/oj

The current `MACHINE_COMPLIANCE_ARTIFACT` class therefore has a better generality basis
than the initial #220 admission alone.

### #250 / #272 — Member-State permitted divergence

**Primary propositions: supported.**  
**Audit disposition: TOLERABLE with a scope caution.**

GDPR Article 8 allows Member States to lower the child-consent age from 16 to no lower
than 13. MiCA Article 143(3) permits Member States to disapply or shorten the CASP
transition. Working Time Directive Article 15 permits more favourable national
worker-protection provisions.

Official sources:

- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1114/oj
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2003/88/oj/eng

The class is well-supported as a **permission-state warning**: the Union text can
expressly leave bounded national divergence unresolved.

The canonical cases must not be read as proving a particular Member State's concrete
national rule merely from the Union permission. Actual national exercise remains separate
evidence. The current definition already says that exercise must be resolved, so no
corpus edit is required.

### #263 — Union-nexus applicability

**Primary propositions: supported.**  
**Audit disposition: TOLERABLE / class survives.**

GDPR Article 3(2) reaches non-EU controllers/processors through offering/monitoring
relationships to persons in the Union. Market Abuse Regulation Article 2 reaches
third-country actions/omissions through the covered-financial-instrument nexus.

Official sources:

- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2014/596/oj

These are genuinely different connector types. The class also contains an explicit
anti-generic boundary: mere location outside the Union is not dispositive, but mere
incidental Union contact is not enough either.

No definition repair is indicated by this sample.

**Primary proposition: strongly supported.**  
**Audit disposition: TOLERABLE / high-value anti-merge case.**

C-715/20 cleanly separates:

2. the separate directly invocable Article 47 Charter route requiring effective judicial

Official source:

- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:62020CJ0715

This is strong evidence that `DIRECTIVE_INVOCABILITY_STATE` and
`EU_PRIMACY_DISAPPLICATION_STATE` are distinct state owners rather than duplicate
labels.

## Admission-threshold drift

The audit does **not** find that later classes were simply admitted more casually.

There are repeated negative controls, in-place revisions, compression attempts and
explicit ordinary-doctrine baselines throughout the run.

However, the **language of the decision rule drifted**:

- #218 treats mature incumbent doctrine as part of the reason a class is unnecessary;
- later admissions routinely state that ordinary doctrine completely owns the law, then
  ask a different question: is there still a bounded reusable representation failure for
  the regression corpus?
- #245 gives the clearest mature version of the rule by rejecting only after the
  candidate decomposes into ordinary scope analysis plus an existing class.

That distinction must be made canonical.

## Path dependence / selection bias

### Coverage gates create a pro-admission prior if left unchecked

#261, #268 and #275 deliberately searched for the “highest-information uncovered
dimension”. That is useful for discovery, but it pre-selects candidates that look unlike
the existing taxonomy.

The later research issue must therefore start from a **null of no new class**. Selection
as a coverage gap is not evidence for admission.

### #271 depth mode was not followed cleanly

#271 explicitly selected depth mode and identified
`DIFFERENTIATED_MEMBER_STATE_PARTICIPATION` as the first definition/evidence mismatch.

Yet #273 remained open while work continued through #272/#274 and then back into a
coverage gate (#275) and a new class (#276). #277 later imposed a generality/composition
lane and #278–#280 completed it, but the mode transition was not explicitly reconciled at
the time.

This is a governance defect, not evidence that #276 or the later classes are wrong.

Future project-mode decisions are binding until an explicit gate supersedes them.

### Anti-growth controls did real work

The record also contains important counter-pressure:

- #244 removed three classes;
- #260, #267 and #277 attempted further compression and preserved negative results;
- #271 recognised falling marginal value of breadth;
- #277 forced three generality/composition-only runs;
- #278–#280 added no classes.

The research program therefore shows path dependence risk, but not uncontrolled
one-way ontology growth.

## Canonical rule repair

The Way of Working is revised with two principles.

### Incumbent / residual-value test

> **Existing is not useless. Different is not valuable. Novel is not demanded.**

An incumbent doctrine, method, competitor or ordinary research rule is the strongest
baseline, not an automatic rejection.

- REJECT when the incumbent plus existing Needle classes **losslessly predicts and
  expresses the consequential failure**, leaving no bounded residual regression value.
- ADMIT/REVISE only when a reusable residual state survives, its boundary is explicit,
  and the class materially helps organise/test regression cases rather than merely
  renaming known doctrine.
- Do not claim novelty because the corpus label is different.
- Do not deny corpus value merely because the underlying law/doctrine is familiar.

### Mode / horizon binding

A Project Health or coverage decision that selects a mode or sole next WIP remains
binding until:

- it completes; or
- a later explicit gate records why it is superseded.

Do not silently abandon depth/compression/generality mode because another attractive
candidate appears.

## Why no compression now

The frozen sample finds no false primary-law foundation and no sampled class that can be
losslessly merged.

The newest classes were also attacked in #267/#277, and #274 provides strong
composition evidence for one of the most tempting merges.

Forcing another merge because class count is 26 would be outcome-seeking.

## Independent Astra red-team

After the retrospective result was drafted, the sponsor supplied one bounded GPT-6
Astra / medium project-level red team. Astra was used as **analysis, not evidence**.

Its verdict was **CONTINUE DEPTH**, but it identified an important failure mode in the
draft rule:

> requiring “lossless” decomposition can create an asymmetric burden of proof that
> protects taxonomy, because almost every legal distinction contains some nuance.

That criticism is accepted.

The canonical repair therefore defines loss only as loss of a **pre-specified
consequential regression distinction**. Descriptive nuance does not protect a class.
Failure to prove redundancy is not positive evidence of class value, and useful cases can
survive class consolidation.

Astra also supplied a useful depth-lane falsifier: if #273 and the next two depth runs,
each selected before substantive research, yield only further illustrations and no
definition change, sharper boundary, consequential regression correction, rejection,
retirement or compression signal, depth has failed to show higher information value and
must return to explicit direction review.

Astra independently selected #273 as the best-supported first depth target. It also
cautioned that Ireland and Denmark inside one Protocol 21/22 setting are contrasting
states, not two independent validations, and that a positive #273 result cannot validate
all Treaty protocols.

These points tighten #273's acceptance criteria; they do not alter the source evidence
or the 79-case / 26-class corpus.
