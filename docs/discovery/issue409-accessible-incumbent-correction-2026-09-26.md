# Issue #409 — accessible-incumbent correction before Candidate-B replay

Date: 2026-09-26

## Trigger

After CBAM was mechanically selected but **before execution**, public market research found a
material comparator omitted from the original access framing:

> **low-cost / niche regulatory point solutions aimed at smaller importers.**

This must strengthen the baseline before any Candidate-B result.

## CBAM point-tool evidence

Public vendor material currently advertises, for example:

- **CBAM Data** — importer plan around EUR 99/month with watched CN/origin pairs and alerts;
- **CBAM Desk** — from roughly EUR 145/month, including maintained EU default values, benchmarks,
  import administration, calculation traces and reporting;
- **Carbonable** — starter around EUR 299/month for small importers;
- other niche tools publicly target SME importers with threshold, price, emissions and reporting
  workflows.

These are vendor claims, not independently validated product-quality evidence.

But they are enough to reject the assumption:

> 'only large enterprises can access maintained CBAM state.'

## Consequence for the original CBAM selection

The frozen CBAM replay v0.1 is:

# **RETIRED_UNEXECUTED — STRONGER ACCESSIBLE INCUMBENT FOUND BEFORE EXECUTION**

Reason:

The proposed Candidate-B thin state sheet would overlap heavily with publicly advertised
small-importer point tools:

- threshold tracking;
- certificate price state;
- default-value state;
- evidence trace;
- reporting history;
- alerts.

Running a spreadsheet-vs-Needle replay while ignoring those products would no longer test the
actual small-team market alternative.

This is not a CBAM product-quality verdict.

It is an experiment-design correction.

## Revised Candidate-B eligibility

Because #409 is specifically an **access** hypothesis, add one condition before selecting a
vertical:

> **No publicly evidenced accessible point solution may already absorb the proposed Needle
> residual well enough that the internal treatment becomes a strawman comparison.**

This condition is frozen before assessing later residuals.

Accessible point solutions receive full credit even when they are not enterprise platforms.

Public marketing does not prove every claimed feature works, but it is sufficient to prevent a
claim of obvious capability/access absence.

## Re-run frozen #377 order under corrected eligibility

### 1. CBAM

**FAIL — ACCESS RESIDUAL NOT ESTABLISHED.**

Reason: multiple publicly marketed low-cost/niche tools already cover much of the proposed thin
state/threshold/price/reporting job.

### 2. CER critical-entity identification

**FAIL — LEAN-TARGET POPULATION NOT YET EVIDENCED.**

The residual is real legal state, but #409 requires a credible smaller/lean actor population.
Do not infer that merely from the Directive.

### 3. CSRD Member-State divergence

**FAIL — CURRENT MANDATORY CORE TARGET IS NOT A LEAN/SME SEGMENT.**

Directive (EU) 2026/470 narrows mandatory reporting to undertakings exceeding EUR 450m turnover
and 1,000 employees, with protected smaller value-chain undertakings.

This is not the first Candidate-B lean-team wedge.

### 4. MDR transition/device/conformity state

**PASS TO DETAILED INCUMBENT CHECK.**

Independent official evidence:

- the EU medical-device sector is strongly SME-shaped;
- Commission evaluation material reports around 90% of EU medtech companies/manufacturers as SMEs;
- SMEs bear disproportionate MDR/IVDR compliance cost;
- transition eligibility depends on more than parent text, including device/cohort state,
  certificate/process conditions and notified-body state;
- EUDAMED became mandatory for key modules from 28 May 2026 and exposes public certificate/status
  information.

Accessible point tools exist for **parts** of the job (for example EUDAMED data/monitoring and
submission support), so MDR is not assumed to be an empty market.

The remaining question is whether those tools cover the **cross-owner transition/applicability
decision** or mostly automate one state owner.

## New immediate successor

# **MDR LEAN-TEAM RESIDUAL / POINT-TOOL CAPABILITY AUDIT**

Before any historical replay:

1. map the exact MDR transition/applicability state needed by a small manufacturer;
2. map official/free sources and EUDAMED;
3. map affordable niche tools aimed at SMEs;
4. identify what, if anything, still requires manual legal composition;
5. run a replay only if a non-strawman residual survives.

## Methodological note

This correction does not cherry-pick a friendlier regime after a null.

No Candidate-B outcome was executed.

The treatment/comparator contract itself became invalid once stronger small-team incumbents were
found.

Strengthening the comparator before execution is mandatory under Needle's operating rules.
