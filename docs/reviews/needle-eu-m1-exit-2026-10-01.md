## M1 journey disposition

For the declared medical coverage, the repository now supports the bounded path:

> ordinary question or exact represented identifier -> source-bound result/locator ->
> bounded overview or represented MDR/IVDR act -> typed time / relationships / evidence ->
> return/deep navigation

The journey is evidence-bounded rather than a general legal-answer service. Exact identifiers
may own deterministic internal navigation; ordinary guidance matching does not infer one act.
Official-source handoffs remain available alongside internal explanation.

## Material findings carried forward

1. EU-level timing cannot be used as a substitute for national implementation/applicability.
2. Legal time is version/amendment aware and can require multiple provision-specific dates.
3. Current state and already-fixed future state must remain distinct.
4. Navigation edges are not legal dependencies or applicability conclusions.
5. A single clean status date can be misleading: WP4 review caught and repaired the IVDR
   collapse between the 9 Jul 2024 transition-amendment effect and 10 Jan 2025 Article 10a
   application date.
6. Retrieval remains deliberately bounded: no-hit/outside-coverage is not evidence that no
   legal rule exists.
