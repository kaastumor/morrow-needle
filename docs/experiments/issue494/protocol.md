## One question, fixed evidence and treatments

Question: **What did the European Parliament record on the selected package decisions concerning consumer-facing environmental claims, and how are the two legislative files related?** A clause-specific support question is a boundary probe, not the question these package ballots answer.

The [evidence ledger](evidence.md) fixes the two files, exactly one selected ballot within each, the relationship, exclusion, unsearched amendments, source identities, counts, supplements and uncertainty. Dates do not delimit a comprehensively searched period. The **linked specimen** and **competent handoff baseline** carry the same factual content, qualifications, direct source links, and substantive reading budget: two selected decision blocks, two eight-row group tables, one relationship/difference, one same-file/exclusion note, coverage and supplement limits. Both permit direct official/provider navigation. The frozen HTML arms expose the same **13 unique external source destinations**; the competent handoff repeats some of those links earlier as part of its source-first navigation, which is a presentation/navigation difference rather than additional evidence. No additional source, hidden member database, political-personalization input or algorithmic selection is allowed in one arm. Layout and navigation may differ. The separately addressable result sections must carry their own package/clause and coverage caveats.

Stance invariance: holding the substantive subject and explicit filters fixed, “I support,” “I oppose” and “I have no position” produce the **same selected decisions, order, explanations and limits**. The static pages collect no stance and have no stance-dependent logic. A changed substantive question may change relevance but is a different treatment.

### Reading questions and corrected answer key

1. **Which text belongs to 17 January 2024?** P9_TA(2024)0018 is the January first-reading text. P9_TA(2023)0201 is May 2023 same-file history. The January manual primary-PDF-text transfer/recount reported in #494 supports the exact January ballot and aggregate distribution; it is not authenticated original bytes or a full independent identity audit.
2. **Are May and January two laws?** No. They are stages/decisions in 2022/0092(COD); only January's selected package ballot is displayed here. The May text must not substitute for the January voted text.
3. **Why include Green Claims and exclude nature restoration?** The Parliament explicitly links the claims files and they share a specific consumer-claims question. January also addresses durability/guarantees; March has explicit-claim substantiation/verification and a different legislative stage. Nature restoration is a broad environmental-topic neighbour, excluded from this claims-specific set. This single control does not validate hard near-neighbour choices.
4. **What do the distributions describe?** Two specified historical roll calls, with for/against/abstained counts per published historical group label. They do not establish every clause's support, motives, present positions, a cross-decision rate, individual switching or national-party positions. NI denotes non-attached members, not a group.
5. **Does arithmetic agreement prove individual-ballot verification?** No. Both decisions have reported primary-text recounts of manually transferred named lists, with distinct checker coverage. Original source bytes/visuals, provider per-member comparison, official IDs, eligibility, historical affiliation and complete correction coverage remain unverified.
6. **What if a language version disagrees?** Preserve the observed discrepancy and adjudicate it against the exact ballot's official evidence owner. Do not vote across translations. The March reported primary count and names support 74 abstentions for that published ballot; the earlier indexed Estonian discrepancy's cause remains unknown.
7. **Does a package vote establish a separate vote on the selected provision?** No. Each result explicitly says it covered the package. Clause-specific attribution requires a separately identified and checked amendment/ballot; none is displayed for the selected provision here.
8. **What within-file ballot coverage is established?** One selected roll call per file. Earlier May proceedings are same-file history. March Am 153 lapsed and Am 46D has an aggregate-only electronic result, not individual group ballots in this display. Other amendments and ballots in either file were not exhaustively searched or individually recorded. The example dates are not a complete interval; missing coverage is unknown, not “no vote.”

These eight questions are exposed development checks, not held-out fixtures. A pass on them is neither a user benefit finding nor an unprimed retrieval finding.

## Frozen pre-run addendum — diagnostic reading pilot

This addendum freezes the first proposed reader pilot **before any participant observation**. It is a small diagnostic comparison, not a population estimate, political-opinion study or authorisation to recruit. Operational details are frozen in [pilot-runbook.md](pilot-runbook.md), with predeclared capture fields in [pilot-observation-template.csv](pilot-observation-template.csv).

- **Sample:** 16 English-reading adults, 8 per arm.
- **Assignment:** between-subject 1:1 assignment to linked specimen or competent handoff. The exact 8/8 allocation is deterministically randomized and frozen in `pilot-runbook.md` using SHA-256 over a fixed seed and participant ID; there is no operator choice after arrivals begin. No crossover in the scored pilot because seeing either arm teaches the shared factual answer.
- **Environment:** desktop/laptop reading only for the scored pilot, with the same source availability in both arms. Mobile/reflow remains a separate browser/accessibility check.
- **Questions:** the eight frozen reading questions below. Questions 1 (exact voted text), 7 (package versus clause) and 8 (within-file ballot coverage) are **critical items**.
- **Recorded outcomes:** answer correctness, explicitly unresolved answers, source openings, navigation errors and completion time. Record these separately; do not collapse them into one score.
- **Political stance:** do not ask for support/opposition, party preference, vote intention or persuasion. The artifact is stance-invariant and the study concerns comprehension/navigation only.
- **Hard stop:** an interface-caused wrong ballot, stage or voted-text attribution blocks the affected output regardless of speed.
- **Pre-specified diagnostic continuation rule:** the linked-specimen arm must not perform worse than the handoff arm on any critical item and must produce at least **3 additional correct critical-item responses** across the 24 critical-item opportunities in its 8-person arm. Otherwise treat the presentation result as parity/insufficient and retain the simpler handoff. This is a pragmatic pilot threshold, **not** a statistical-significance claim.
- **Recruitment/execution:** still requires separate authorisation. Fix recruitment source, randomisation procedure, exact browser/device setup and timing instructions before the first participant; do not choose them after observing outcomes.
