# Issue #409 — mechanical lean-team vertical selection

Date: 2026-09-26

Selection follows the frozen #377 residual order and #409 eligibility rule.

## Eligibility conditions

A residual is eligible only if all are supported before the replay:

1. credible smaller/lean-team actor population is independently evidenced;
2. a non-parent-text owner can materially change the operative answer;
3. public authoritative state is sufficient for reproducible internal proxy work;
4. confidential customer facts are not required;
5. the job is narrower than generic EU monitoring.

## Residual 1 — CBAM

### Condition 1 — lean/smaller actor population

**PASS.**

Official Commission/impact-assessment material explicitly treats SME importers as materially
affected by CBAM compliance design.

Evidence:

- Regulation (EU) 2025/2083 introduced a 50-tonne de minimis threshold and exempts roughly 90%
  of importers;
- Commission material says the exempted small-quantity population is largely SMEs/individuals;
- the 2025 impact assessment for downstream expansion estimates roughly 45–60% of additional
  importers would be SMEs depending on option, explicitly excluding below-threshold importers;
- the simplification impact assessment identifies thousands of importers clustered near the
  50-tonne threshold.

Sources:
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/en/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32025R2083
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A52025SC0058
- https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A52025SC0988

Interpretation:

> CBAM is not only a large-enterprise regime. Small/medium importers are an explicit policy and
> burden population, although the 50-tonne exemption removes many of the smallest from the
> definitive obligations.

### Condition 2 — independent operative-state owner

**PASS.**

#377 already froze CBAM as a residual because operational calculation depends on dynamic
authoritative inputs beyond parent-text amendment history.

Current official example:

- Commission publishes authoritative CBAM certificate prices;
- 2026 prices are quarterly;
- from 2027 prices become weekly;
- the price is a Commission-calculated weighted average of ETS auction clearing prices.

Source:
- https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism/price-cbam-certificates_en

### Condition 3 — public authoritative replay state

**PASS.**

Public sources expose:

- the definitive-regime rule;
- 50-tonne threshold;
- Commission/NCA responsibilities;
- certificate-price publications;
- sector/default-value/guidance surfaces;
- public implementation chronology.

### Condition 4 — no confidential customer facts required

**PASS for proxy.**

The internal proxy can use an **externally evidenced boundary-importer archetype** rather than
pretend to be a real customer.

The Commission impact assessment states that roughly 2,700 importers sit within ±15% of the
50-tonne threshold.

A frozen transaction/mass scenario can therefore be anchored to a documented real population.

This remains a proxy and cannot prove real-user value.

### Condition 5 — bounded job

**PASS.**

Scope can be limited to:

> one EU importer profile, selected CBAM goods, threshold/applicability state, current
> certificate-price state and immediate compliance timeline.

No general regulatory-monitoring platform is required.

## Mechanical result

CBAM is the **first eligible residual** in the frozen #377 order.

Per the predeclared selection rule, later residuals (CER/CSRD/MDR/IVDR/CPR/Gas Appliances) are not
considered for the first #409 replay merely because some may look more attractive.
