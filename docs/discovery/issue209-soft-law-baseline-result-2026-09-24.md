# #209 result — soft-law effect family vs established EU doctrine

Date: 2026-09-24  
Issue: #209  
Claim type: `CORRECTNESS_VALIDITY / FAILURE_FAMILY_DISTINCTIVENESS`

## Decision

# **REJECT**

The parked Cycle-3 candidate:

> `OFFICIAL_SOFT_LAW_EFFECT_COLLAPSE`

does not earn a Needle-specific failure family.

The underlying legal phenomenon is real. The proposed class is not sufficiently
predictive beyond mature EU soft-law doctrine.

## Strong baseline

Representative sources include:

- CJEU Case C-911/19, EBA Guidelines:
  https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=CELEX:62019CJ0911
- EBA Article-16 compliance framework:
  https://www.eba.europa.eu/about-us/legal-and-policy-framework/compliance-eba-regulatory-products
- EU overview of legislation/recommendations/opinions:
  https://european-union.europa.eu/institutions-law-budget/law/types-legislation_en
- current scholarship on EBA guidelines/recommendations as formally soft acts
  with substantial legal/practical effects.

C-911/19 already supplies the ordinary effect-analysis rule: examine the act's
substance, content, context and the powers of the issuing body rather than infer
all consequences from legal form alone.

## Mapping the #178 profiles

### EBA Q&A

The no-binding-force / no-comply-or-explain / practical-supervisory-significance
profile is directly resolved by source-role and legal-basis analysis.

### ESA Article 16 guidelines

C-911/19 itself distinguishes lack of binding legal effects as such from the
statutory make-every-effort / comply-or-explain context.

### Commission competition guidelines

Self-binding administrative practice, equal-treatment and
legitimate-expectation consequences arise from established doctrine specific to
the institution/instrument/context.

### Low-effect official information

The #178 ECHA control is naturally excluded where the official material lacks a
stronger legally recognised effect.

## Shared-mechanism test

The only common statement left is:

> one BINDING/NONBINDING bit is too coarse to encode every legally relevant
> effect of every official non-legislative artifact.

That is true but too generic to be a useful Needle failure family.

To make the class predictive, Needle would need durable dimensions such as:

- comply-or-explain;
- make-every-effort duties;
- self-binding administrative practice;
- equal treatment;
- legitimate expectations;
- interpretive relevance;
- supervisory/practical significance.

Those dimensions are produced by materially different legal bases and doctrines.

Adding them merely to save the class would recreate the effect-ontology pressure
that #183 explicitly warned against.

## Kill rule

Established EU soft-law doctrine plus ordinary source-role analysis already
explains:

- why a binary binding bit is insufficient;
- each #178 effect profile;
- the low-effect boundary;
- the risk of over- or under-promoting an instrument.

No missing reusable distinction survives.

## Consequence

Do not create:

- `OFFICIAL_SOFT_LAW_EFFECT_COLLAPSE`;
- a soft-law effect ontology;
- guidance/Q&A ingestion or monitoring;
- fresh validation merely to reconfirm established doctrine.

The #178 examples remain useful legal-research background examples.

## Astra decision

Not triggered.

The proposed structural tie did not survive normal red teaming: the common layer
is generic coarse-representation loss while the legally material effects remain
doctrine-specific. Astra must not be used to manufacture distinctiveness.
