## 1. Why MDR survives the lean-team screen

### SME-shaped sector

European Commission material describes the EU medical-device sector as strongly SME-driven.

Recent Commission evaluation material reports:

- over 38,000 manufacturers;
- around 90% of medtech companies/manufacturers are SMEs;
- SMEs and niche-device manufacturers bear disproportionate compliance cost.

Official sources:
- https://health.ec.europa.eu/medical-devices-sector/overview_en
- Commission MDR/IVDR evaluation / SWD 2025 materials.

Therefore this is not a hypothetical 'small company might care' segment.

### Real cross-owner transition state

MDR legacy-device transition eligibility is not one current-text lookup.

Public Commission material identifies conditions including:

- device must be within an eligible legacy-device cohort;
- continued compliance with prior legislation;
- no significant design/intended-purpose change;
- no unacceptable health/safety risk;
- MDR QMS in place by the required deadline;
- formal MDR conformity-assessment application by the required deadline;
- written agreement with an MDR-designated notified body by the required deadline;
- appropriate surveillance / notified-body responsibility;
- class/device type determines final transition endpoint.

Commission transition timeline highlights, for many MDR legacy devices:

- 26 May 2024 — application + MDR QMS deadline;
- 26 September 2024 — written notified-body agreement deadline;
- 31 December 2027 / 31 December 2028 — different end dates by device class/type.

Sources:
- https://health.ec.europa.eu/document/download/06bb3982-dc01-46aa-95af-d8833c9fcb21_en
- https://health.ec.europa.eu/medical-devices-sector/new-regulations/guidance-mdcg-endorsed-documents-and-other-guidance_en

## 2. Official/public state owner

EUDAMED is now a material live owner.

From 28 May 2026 the first four modules became mandatory, including:

- Actor registration;
- UDI/device registration;
- Notified Bodies & Certificates;
- Market Surveillance.

The public certificate module records certificate states including issued/amended/suspended/
reinstated/withdrawn/refused and restrictions.

Sources:
- https://health.ec.europa.eu/medical-devices-eudamed/overview_en
- https://health.ec.europa.eu/medical-devices-eudamed/notified-bodies-and-certificates-module_en

Important:

> EUDAMED is one owner, not necessarily the full legal conclusion.

## 3. Affordable point-tool correction

Small manufacturers are not limited to manual EUDAMED browsing.

Public point tools advertise accessible services such as:

- EUDAMED record preparation/validation;
- certificate monitoring and expiry alerts;
- supplier/device portfolio checks;
- recurring registry monitoring;
- relatively low-volume SME pricing.

Examples:
- EudaMed Check — public per-report and annual SME-oriented monitoring plans;
- EUDAMate — self-serve device preparation and optional registry-care plans;
- Lets EUDAMED — SME-oriented EUDAMED preparation tiers.

Public sources:
- https://report.eudamed.com/pricing.cfm
- https://www.eudamate.eu/pricing
- https://lets-eudamed.eu/tarifs?lang=en

These are vendor claims and are not independently validated.

They nevertheless prevent Needle from claiming a simple 'SMEs cannot monitor EUDAMED' gap.

## 4. What remains after subtracting the point tools

The surviving Candidate-B question is not:

> can Needle monitor a certificate or prepare EUDAMED data?

Those jobs are already visibly occupied.

The plausible residual is:

> **Can a lean manufacturer maintain the legal conclusion 'this legacy device may continue to be
> placed on the market until date T' when that conclusion depends on a conjunction of legal cohort,
> device class, prior certificate state, design/purpose continuity, risk state, QMS/application/
> agreement milestones and live notified-body/certificate state?**

This is a cross-owner **operative eligibility** job.

### Candidate-B minimal state

A useful treatment would likely need only:

- device / cohort identity;
- applicable transition endpoint;
- required condition set;
- condition status + evidence owner;
- governing date;
- certificate/notified-body state;
- unresolved condition;
- resulting eligibility conclusion;
- next state/check date.

This is not a new schema yet.
