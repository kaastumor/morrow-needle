### 8 — C-15/26 Boehringer Ingelheim Vetmedica

**Classification: INDEPENDENT_OPERATIVE_STATE**

This is the sole strong residual in the frozen sample.

Article 3(d) of Regulation (EC) No 469/2009 asks whether the veterinary marketing
authorisation is the first authorisation to place the product on the market as a medicinal
product even though an earlier marketing authorisation for the same active ingredient for
human use had already been granted under Directive 2001/83.

The practical SPC analysis therefore depends on the existence, identity, sequence and legal
status of **authoritative marketing-authorisation decisions** created under separate
regulatory regimes.

Those authorisations are not merely words in Regulation 469/2009 and are not an amendment
history of that Regulation. They are public-law administrative states that can be created at
different times and whose ordering changes the Article 3(d) answer while the parent SPC rule
remains unchanged.

Omitting or projecting the earlier authorisation state can therefore reverse the practical
answer.

This survives the anti-inflation test.

Post-classification Needle fit:

> closest to `DYNAMIC_REFERENCE_STATUS` / authoritative external determination concepts,
> but no corpus/class change is proposed.

Official case:
https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/C/2026/2871/oj

## Red team of the single positive

C-15/26 must not be overclaimed.

### Alternative explanation: this is just a case fact

The earlier marketing authorisation is indeed a fact in the litigation record.

If "legal-state" simply meant "a legally relevant fact exists", nearly every case would
qualify.

Why it survives:

- the fact is not an ordinary private event;
- it is an authoritative public-law authorisation created under a separate regulated legal
  process;
- its identity and sequence are directly referenced by the EU SPC criterion;
- the parent SPC text can remain unchanged while the authorisation state changes;
- substituting/omitting the earlier authorisation can reverse the Article 3(d) result.

The residual is therefore narrower than matter facts generally.

### Alternative explanation: ordinary lawyers always check this

Very plausible.

Nothing in Phase 3B shows that Needle is needed to notice or reconstruct the authorisation
history.

The CJEU reference itself surfaces the issue explicitly.

Therefore C-15/26 is evidence about **legal-state structure**, not about latent detection,
research burden, product value or Needle advantage.

### Alternative explanation: one in twelve is noise

This sample is not statistically representative, so neither 1/12 nor 0/12 would support a
population-frequency estimate.

The relevant project conclusion is not "8.3% prevalence".

It is:

> a second independently sourced frame did not reproduce the high residual rate observed in
> the deliberately stratified Phase-2 stress sample.

That weakens the prevalence thesis.

## Scientific conclusion

The evidence supports:

> **Some EU-law questions genuinely depend on independently moving operative state that
> cannot be reduced to current text plus ordinary amendment/cross-instrument analysis.**

The evidence does **not** support:

> **That residual is common across real EU-law research or implementation work.**

The project has now tested prevalence in two externally sourced frames with different
selection pressures and failed to obtain support for a broad prevalence claim.

Continuing by searching for another external frame would increasingly risk serial
result-seeking.

## Consequence for Needle

This result actually strengthens the interpretation of the frozen corpus as an
**adversarial known-failure reference asset**:

- the corpus is intentionally enriched for unusual, consequential state failures;
- it should not be interpreted as a frequency map of ordinary EU-law work;
- its classes can remain valuable regression/reference distinctions even when their
  population prevalence is low or unmeasured.

That is consistent with Needle's current smaller identity.

No class, case or Reference Pack change is earned.

## Final disposition

# **EU_LEGAL_STATE_EXISTENCE_SUPPORTED — PREVALENCE_NOT_SUPPORTED**

Recommended allocation:

> **PARK the EU prevalence programme at this boundary and return WIP to the preserved
> legal-AI evaluation-science / evaluation-contract research front.**

EU legal-state research remains reopenable from a genuinely new external trigger, such as:

- measured burden in a legal/regulatory workflow;
- a commercial/system contract showing a concrete representational omission;
- independently observed failures where current-text/change tooling produced a wrong answer;
- a new public corpus that supplies a materially different source population.

Do not reopen merely by finding another case family likely to contain stateful examples.
