## Why none is a diagnostic rescue

The frozen definition required all three:

1. R makes the pre-sealed consequential error;
2. C avoids it;
3. C explicitly/evidentially identifies and applies the decisive distinction.

No R arm made the consequential error.

Therefore richer explanation, extra citations, additional caution or more
detailed legal framing in a C answer cannot manufacture a rescue.

## Red-team interpretation

### “The cases were too easy”

Rejected as a post-hoc escape route.

The compiler had to accept the first eligible candidate under the frozen family
queue, realism tests and search budgets. Baseline success on all three
adversarial tasks is an experiment result, not permission to relabel the tasks
defective or replace them.

### “C may still have been more cautious”

The Astra-revised claim already treated corpus access + adversarial framing +
induced attention as the C treatment package.

No consequential correctness/detection difference was observed.

### “The model may already know these kinds of traps”

Retained limitation.

R means no **supplied** Needle corpus. Public training exposure cannot be ruled
out. The pilot therefore does not establish population equivalence or corpus
uselessness.

It does establish non-demonstration of added workflow value under the frozen
conditions.

### “Try more or harder tasks”

Forbidden.

#210 pre-registered a one-shot 3+1 challenge set and explicitly prohibited adding
harder cases after parity.

### Secondary efficiency

Not used to rescue the null.

No post-result source-count, verbosity, time or citation-quality metric can
reverse the frozen primary outcome.
