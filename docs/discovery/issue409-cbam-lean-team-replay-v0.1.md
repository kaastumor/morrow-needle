# Issue #409 — CBAM lean-team historical maintenance replay v0.1

Status: **FROZEN BEFORE EXECUTION**

Date: 2026-09-26

## Purpose

Test whether a narrow maintained operative-state representation plausibly reduces repeated
research/reopening for a lean CBAM importer compared with a strong ordinary lightweight workflow.

This is an internal proxy.

It cannot establish willingness to pay, real legal-practitioner acceptance or actual lawyer-hour
savings.

## Why CBAM

CBAM is the first eligible #377 residual under the predeclared #409 selection order.

Strong anti-Needle evidence is built in:

- the 50-tonne simplification removed roughly 90% of importers from definitive obligations;
- official CBAM guidance is unusually rich;
- the Commission and customs systems already monitor important threshold/authorisation state;
- if ordinary public guidance + retained notes is sufficient, Candidate B should fail.

## Externally evidenced actor archetype

Do not claim a real customer.

The proxy represents:

> an EU-established SME importer of Annex-I CBAM aluminium goods, operating without an enterprise
> regulatory-intelligence platform and importing close to the 50-tonne annual threshold.

Why this archetype is externally grounded:

- Commission impact analysis identified roughly 2,700 importers within ±15% of the 50-tonne
  threshold;
- Commission CBAM work explicitly treats SMEs as a material importer population;
- downstream-extension impact analysis expects substantial SME participation above the 50-tonne
  threshold as well.

## Frozen transaction scenario

For experimental clarity only:

- goods are stipulated to be covered Annex-I aluminium goods;
- importer is EU-established;
- 2026 cumulative net mass:
  - end Q1: **18 t**;
  - end Q2: **34 t**;
  - 20 September 2026: **55 t**;
- no carbon price paid in country of origin is assumed;
- use Commission default emissions values where a concrete emissions method is needed;
- no confidential firm facts are required.

The invented quantities are an internal test scenario, not evidence about a specific company.

## Frozen historical window

> **20 October 2025 -> 26 September 2026**

Predeclared state checkpoints:

### T0 — 20 October 2025

Regulation (EU) 2025/2083 enters into force.

Question:

> What determines whether this importer is exempt or in scope for 2026?

### T1 — 14 January 2026

Definitive regime is operating.

Question:

> Given expected annual imports above 50 t, what immediate authorisation/monitoring obligations
> should the importer treat as live?

### T2 — 7 April 2026

Commission publishes the first 2026 quarterly CBAM certificate price.

Question:

> What authoritative price applies to Q1 2026 imports, and when is it financially actioned?

### T3 — 6 July 2026

Commission publishes the Q2 price.

Question:

> What changed for Q2, and what state can be carried forward without reopening the full legal
> framework?

### T4 — 26 September 2026

Current observation point.

Question:

> The scenario has crossed 50 t. What operative state is known now, what remains future/unpublished,
> and what actions/timeline matter next?

Q3 2026 certificate price is intentionally not yet available at T4; official publication is
scheduled after quarter end. Correct unresolved/future state is a required outcome.

## Strong lean-team baseline

The baseline may use:

- all relevant public official sources;
- browser/search;
- Commission guidance/FAQs/newsletters;
- ordinary spreadsheet or notes;
- a capable general source-grounded AI assistant;
- retained prior answers, notes and source links;
- reasonable manual update reminders.

The baseline may improve its notes over time.

Do not forbid it from discovering a sensible table or checklist.

The baseline may **not** be silently given:

- paid enterprise regulatory inventory;
- enterprise applicability engine;
- enterprise change-management workflow;
- a pre-populated Needle state model.

## Candidate-B treatment

Use only a thin maintained state sheet, not Full Needle.

Provisional columns, not a new canonical schema:

- `state_key`;
- `current_value`;
- `applies_if`;
- `effective_from` / governing event;
- `authoritative_owner`;
- `source_ref`;
- `last_checked`;
- `next_expected_change`;
- `status`: `KNOWN | FUTURE | UNRESOLVED | NOT_APPLICABLE`.

Start with only the state keys required by the T0 answer.

Add a state key later only if a checkpoint actually requires it.

Likely state families may include:

- annual mass threshold;
- actor authorisation requirement;
- cumulative/import-year applicability;
- certificate price by quarter;
- declaration/surrender deadline;
- purchase availability timeline;
- emissions-value method/source;
- verification availability/status.

These are hypotheses, not required fields.

## Fairness rule

Candidate B gets credit only when the maintained state sheet avoids consequential repeated
reconstruction or preserves a time/state distinction the ordinary baseline actually loses.

Do not count:

- prettier formatting;
- more metadata;
- a field that merely copies an official page;
- fewer browser clicks achieved by hiding setup cost;
- information the baseline already retained correctly.

## Measures

Per checkpoint record:

- official sources opened/reopened;
- distinct authoritative owners consulted;
- prior notes/state reused;
- new state discovered;
- stale state corrected;
- consequential omission/error;
- future/unresolved state handled safely;
- baseline note maintenance operations;
- Candidate-B state maintenance operations;
- number of prior facts that can be carried forward without revalidation;
- whether a source must be reopened because its state is dynamic.

Cumulative:

- setup operations;
- total source reopenings;
- total state-maintenance operations;
- repeated-query reconstruction steps;
- consequential errors;
- historical/current-state contamination;
- false confidence about unpublished/future values.

Do not fabricate minutes.

## Key falsifier

> If the ordinary baseline naturally converges on an equally compact maintained spreadsheet and
> answers all five checkpoints with comparable reopening/maintenance burden, return
> `LEAN_BASELINE_SUFFICIENT`.

That does not erase the wider SME burden; it means this Candidate-B treatment does not solve it.

## Positive threshold

`LEAN_TEAM_ACCESS_GAP_SUPPORTED` requires all of:

1. the lean baseline has material repeated reopening/reconstruction or a consequential state error;
2. Candidate B avoids that issue using only the thin state layer;
3. Candidate-B setup/maintenance does not simply shift equal or greater work earlier;
4. the advantage concerns operative-state maintenance, not generic monitoring already rejected by
   #407;
5. the pattern appears at more than one checkpoint/state owner.

## Execution limitation

A same-session internal replay is contamination-prone.

Preferred execution is isolated/fresh baseline and Candidate-B runs using the frozen scenario.

If isolation is unavailable, perform only a structural workflow replay and label the result
`PUBLIC_PROXY_INSUFFICIENT_EXTERNAL_USER_REQUIRED` rather than pretending causal evidence.

## No automatic build

A positive replay earns only:

> a disposable Candidate-B prototype / user test design.

It does not earn:

- Full Needle;
- generic EU monitoring;
- a new ontology;
- a paid product claim.
