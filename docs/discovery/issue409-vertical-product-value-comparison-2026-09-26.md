## CPR-2024 — product-family migration between CPR-2011 and CPR-2024

### Job / pain

Strong lean-team fit.

Commission material states that CPR-2011 and CPR-2024 will coexist for a significant period and
that **product families migrate individually**. If a product remains covered by a CPR-2011
standard citation, CPR-2011 generally applies; once a new performance harmonised standard is cited
under CPR-2024, the product family moves to the new framework.

The Commission currently expects the first new mandatory performance standards, probably cement,
in 2027.

Construction is heavily SME-shaped.

### Accessible incumbents

Real and strengthening.

Public products such as Certivo claim:

- product-family mapping;
- harmonised-standard sync;
- coexistence-period tracking;
- portfolio alerts;
- CPR documentation workflows.

Other CPR/DPP products also exist.

### Candidate-B residual

Potentially attractive:

> a narrow decision-ready state: **which CPR regime applies to this product family today, which
> OJ/harmonised-technical-specification state owns the answer, what event will flip it, and what
> changes when it flips?**

This closely matches Needle's historical dynamic-set/time/provenance capabilities.

### Limitation

The most consequential CPR-2024 family migrations are still ahead. A clean historical migration
replay is not yet available for the new regime.

### Status

> **NOT_YET_EVENTFUL — RETAIN AS STRONG FORWARD VERTICAL**

Re-open when the first CPR-2024 performance harmonised standard is cited / made mandatory.

## GAR — harmonised-standard legal status / formal objection

### Job / pain

Narrower market than MDR/CPR, but the legal-state event is real and current.

Manufacturers are responsible for identifying applicable EU rules and harmonised standards.
Harmonised-standard references obtain their legal effect through OJ publication; formal objections
can maintain, restrict, remove or prevent citation.

### Current real event

Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1750 of 20 July 2026 decided **not to publish** the
references to:

- EN 17476:2021/A1:2022;
- EN 497:2022

under the Gas Appliances Regulation.

Therefore the mere existence/adoption of EN 497:2022 does not give it GAR presumption-of-conformity
status.

### Strong free baseline

The Commission itself provides:

- harmonised-standard lists;
- formal-objection page;
- OJ/EUR-Lex decisions;
- RSS subscription.

Basic monitoring is therefore **not** a Needle residual.

### Accessible commercial incumbents

General product-compliance / regulatory-intelligence products increasingly claim:

- OJ standards sync;
- product relevance mapping;
- historical status;
- evidence-backed alerts.

Examples include Certivo, Foresight, ComplyMatrix and Grecta Standards Watch.

### Candidate-B residual

The smallest plausible residual is not alerting.

It is a **decision-ready legal status**:

> standard exists / OJ reference state / restriction-or-refusal state / effective date / scope /
> presumption-of-conformity consequence / official evidence / historical as-of answer.

That state can be tested now against authoritative public evidence.

### Reuse of historical Needle capital

Needle already has an earned canonical owner for this exact causal shape:

> `authoritative-dynamic-set-v0.1`

It was originally proven on Toy Safety harmonised-standard reference status plus an orthogonal REACH
Candidate List case.

Therefore GAR requires **no new ontology**.

### Status

> **OCCUPIED_BUT_TESTABLE — BEST IMMEDIATE INTERNAL WEDGE**

Selection means 'best immediate internal proof workload', not 'largest market'.
