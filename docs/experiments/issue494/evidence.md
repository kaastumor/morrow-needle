# #494 selection and evidence ledger — frozen 29 September 2026

## Question and two independent selections

Question: **What did the European Parliament record on the selected package decisions concerning consumer-facing environmental claims, and how are the two legislative files related?** This does not ask who supported a particular clause. A package ballot cannot answer that clause question without separately established amendment evidence.

**File selection:** The anchor is consumer information for the green transition, 2022/0092(COD), later Directive (EU) 2024/825. Green Claims, 2023/0085(COD), is admitted as a separately identified file because the [Parliament's January announcement](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/news/en/press-room/20240112IPR16772) connects them and the [March announcement](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/news/en/press-room/20240308IPR19001/parliament-wants-to-improve-consumer-protection-against-misleading-claims) describes a related consumer-claims mechanism. The January package also covers durability and guarantee information; the March proposal addresses substantiation, communication and verification of explicit environmental claims. The relation is navigation/exploration, **not equivalent political-position measurement**. Nature restoration 2022/0195(COD) is excluded from this claims-specific set: its ecosystem/habitat restoration mechanism is a broad-topic connection. This is one easy exclusion control, not evidence of robust near-neighbour selection.

**Within-file ballot selection:** Exactly one reported plenary roll call is displayed from each admitted file: the 17 January 2024 provisional-agreement ballot, **Am 91**, for 2022/0092(COD), and the 12 March 2024 Commission-proposal ballot after amendments for 2023/0085(COD). Their dates are example dates, **not a searched interval**. Earlier 11 May 2023 proceedings in the January file are identified as same-file history, not displayed as an additional vote or separate law. The March source-inspected controls include lapsed Am 153 and aggregate-only Am 46D, but neither supplies an individual roll call for this display. Other amendments and decisions in either file were **not exhaustively searched or individually recorded**; they are omitted by specimen design. The provider methodology says amendment results are not displayed on its site, while the current March vote page exposes a “View all roll-call votes, including amendments” link. That destination and its coverage were not followed in this pass, so no absence claim follows. Unknown coverage is not “no other vote.”

The file selection was exploratory and partly exposed to outcomes; the January example was already known, and the March overall result appeared during scoping. The two decisions were selected manually, not by a general similarity algorithm. No cross-decision total, rate, political alignment or comprehensive track record is supported.
