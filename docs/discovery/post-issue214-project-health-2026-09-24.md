## Direction

# **SIMPLIFY — IDLE BY DESIGN**

The project should not answer a zero-rescue value gate by inventing a new value
gate.

#214 produced no demonstrated diagnostic advantage for offering the frozen
adversarial index + its adversarial-use instruction over ordinary source-grounded
QA on the sealed challenge set.

All three adversarial R/C pairs were correct and explicitly applied the decisive
distinction. The matched control also produced pass/pass without a material
C-only complication.

The pre-frozen interpretation therefore contracts the active project identity.

## Smallest surviving identity

# **Needle = adversarial legal-research regression/reference corpus + minimal evaluation discipline**

This is smaller than the pre-#214 identity.

The corpus remains useful for:

- preserving real exposed legal-information failure mechanisms;
- regression/reference work;
- teaching/review examples;
- future calibration where exposure is explicit;
- supplying a disciplined history of what was tested and falsified.

The evaluation discipline remains useful for:

- exposure classification;
- preregistration when a genuinely new claim warrants testing;
- sealing;
- strong baselines;
- preservation of parity/negative results.

It is no longer supported as an active general diagnostic-workflow advantage.

## What #214 adds to prior evidence

The contraction is cumulative rather than based on one null:

- #88: Method did not outperform the baseline on surfaced traps;
- #97: Method did not outperform on four fresh latent-trap tasks;
- #203: current corpus/protocol treatment produced no decision-changing
  consequence on the surfaced OPP-115 conflict and remained unresolved;
- #207/#209: two attractive taxonomy reserves disappeared under stronger
  incumbent concepts;
- #214: offering the frozen corpus index + adversarial framing produced zero
  diagnostic rescues on three fresh latent adversarial tasks.

The pattern does not prove Needle-specific machinery can never help.

It does remove the evidence-backed reason to keep actively searching for a
general workflow advantage.

## What remains valuable

### Corpus/reference asset

29 exposed cases / 15 trap classes remain coherent as an adversarial
regression/reference collection.

Do not call them fresh validation again.

### Research discipline

The project has repeatedly improved by:

- using stronger boring baselines;
- admitting contamination;
- recording negative findings;
- deleting candidate classes;
- refusing product/schema growth after nulls.

That discipline remains worth preserving even when the project itself becomes
smaller.

### Real-use reopening

A future hypothesis may reopen only when an external event changes the boundary,
for example:

- a real investigator independently reports that a corpus example prevented a
  consequential error;
- a recurring handoff/review job demonstrates material reconstruction savings;
- a genuinely new legal-information failure appears during ordinary work and
  survives incumbent-concept first refusal;
- an external benchmark/user adopts the corpus/protocol and exposes a concrete
  unmet need.

Do not manufacture those triggers through standing search.

## Discovery lane

Change from **active linked discovery** to **event-triggered discovery only**.

Fresh legal research may still yield a corpus candidate, but the project should
not maintain a standing hunt merely to keep the corpus growing.

The admission test remains strict:

1. real external question/event first;
2. strong incumbent-concept first refusal;
3. consequential distinction;
4. fresh evidence;
5. no automatic architecture/product implication.
