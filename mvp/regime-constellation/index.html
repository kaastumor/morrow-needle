Change review

What should be re-opened when a represented legal node changes?

A review candidate is not a conclusion that its legal effect changed. Propagation requires a verified typed relationship plus overlap with the provision or mechanism that changed. Family membership or graph proximity alone does not propagate review.

Structural-gap candidate

None asserted

No structural absence claim is supported by this bounded projection

The current slice is intentionally incomplete. An enabling power to adopt a measure is not automatically a duty to adopt one, and an absent graph node cannot establish that an expected legal measure does not exist.

Threshold before any future signal: a mandatory expectation, an identifiable target measure, a sufficiently complete official-source search, and no evidence that the requirement was satisfied another way.

Review-gap note: no current claim-level REVIEW_GAP is asserted either. That signal would require a dependent claim's own reviewed-through time plus a later upstream change; the macro prototype does not yet own that evidence at claim level.

Research signals

What a missing or stale connection may mean

The constellation can support review queues, but a missing node is never automatically a legal gap.

Coverage gap

This prototype intentionally expands only representative child acts. The Commission overview is broader than this slice.

Evidence gap

A relationship appears relevant but has not yet been verified strongly enough to display as legal fact.

Review gap

An upstream act changed after a dependent claim was last reviewed. That means “recheck”, not “the child is invalid”.

Structural-gap candidate

An authoritative provision appears to require or enable a downstream measure, but no matching child has yet been identified after bounded source review.

No legal gap is inferred from a missing node. This v0.1 view is a manually bounded research projection, not a complete database of EU medical-device law.
