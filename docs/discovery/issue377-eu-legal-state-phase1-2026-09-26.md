# Issue #377 — EU legal-state outside-in Phase 1 quick run

Date: 2026-09-26  
Mode: **DISCOVER — EU LEGAL-STATE OUTSIDE-IN**  
Status: **PHASE 1 QUICK RUN COMPLETE; PHASE 2 GATE OPEN**

## Question

On contemporary EU regulatory questions selected for sector/instrument breadth rather than
Needle-class fit, is a safe operative answer usually owned by current parent text/change
history alone, or does it require reconstructing independently moving legal state?

This run is **not** a Needle-vs-baseline evaluation and does not test commercial value.

## Selection

The six regimes were frozen in Issue #377 before detailed case research:

1. AI Act — digital / directly applicable regulation;
2. NIS2 — cybersecurity / directive + national transposition;
3. DORA — financial services / delegated + implementing acts;
4. EUDR — environment/supply chain / amended staged applicability;
5. CBAM — climate/trade / definitive regime + dynamic implementing values;
6. gas-appliance harmonised standards — product law / OJ citation state.

The operator already knows the Needle taxonomy, so this is not blind discovery. Bias is
reduced only by the outside-in sector/instrument selection and by freezing the regimes before
class mapping.

## Interpretation states

- **TEXT_SUFFICIENT** — current authoritative parent text safely owns the answer;
- **CHANGE_HISTORY_SUFFICIENT** — parent text plus ordinary amendment/version history safely
  owns it;
- **MULTI_OWNER_STATE_REQUIRED** — at least one independently moving legal-state owner beyond
  parent text/history is needed;
- **INDETERMINATE** — evidence is insufficient.

These are descriptive classifications, not wins.

## Case 1 — AI Act transparency obligation

### Practical question

On 26 September 2026, does Article 50 require a provider of a chatbot intended to interact
directly with natural persons to inform them that they are interacting with AI?

### Minimum authoritative chain

- Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 Article 50(1);
- Article 113 application schedule.

Article 113 makes the Regulation generally applicable from 2 August 2026, with specified
exceptions. Article 50 sits in Chapter IV and is not one of those delayed exceptions.

The Commission's July 2026 Article-50 guidelines independently confirm that the Article 50
transparency obligations apply from 2 August 2026.

### Classification

**TEXT_SUFFICIENT**

For this deliberately basic operative question, the parent Regulation itself owns the
answer. The guidelines are useful interpretation/navigation, not necessary to establish the
basic start date.

A harder legacy-system/grace-period question could require later guidance or amendments, but
changing the question after inspection would defeat this control.

### Needle mapping after freeze

A staged application date resembles existing temporal/application concepts, but no Needle
class is needed to answer this question and no residual Needle value is observed.

- Commission, Guidelines on transparency obligations, 20 July 2026:
  https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/guidelines-transparency-obligations-providers-and-deployers-ai-systems

## Case 2 — NIS2 in the Netherlands

### Practical question

On 26 September 2026, are the Dutch national measures implementing NIS2 operative, or is the
Netherlands still only in the earlier Commission infringement/non-transposition state?

### Minimum authoritative chain

- Directive (EU) 2022/2555 Article 41 required adoption/publication by 17 October 2024 and
  application from 18 October 2024;
- the Commission Netherlands NIS2 country page still displays the 7 May 2025 reasoned opinion
  and states a last update of 7 July 2025;
- EUR-Lex national implementing-measure records show the Dutch Cyberbeveiligingswet and
  Cyberbeveiligingsbesluit published/notified in July 2026;
- the Dutch official Staatsblad Cyberbeveiligingsbesluit Article 35 sets entry into force of
  both the Act and Decree at **15 August 2026**.

### Classification

**MULTI_OWNER_STATE_REQUIRED**

The Directive and an older Commission implementation-status page are insufficient for the
current operative Dutch answer. The national legal act and its commencement rule own the
decisive state.

This is a particularly strong source-view warning because an official Commission page can
remain useful while being temporally behind the national primary-law state it describes.

### Needle mapping after freeze

Existing **SOURCE_VIEW_TEMPORAL_DIVERGENCE** is a strong fit. The earlier
OFFICIAL_TRACKER_UPDATE_LAG family was merged into that class.

This does not count as fresh class validation: the operator knew that taxonomy and Needle
already contains NIS2 tracker-lag research.

## Case 3 — DORA major ICT-incident reporting

### Practical question

For a financial entity under DORA on 26 September 2026, what is the legal deadline for the
initial notification after an ICT incident has been classified as major?

### Minimum authoritative chain

- Regulation (EU) 2022/2554 Article 19 creates the reporting obligation;
- Article 20 empowers technical standards to specify content and time limits;
- Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/301 Article 5 sets the operative deadline:
  as early as possible, in any event within four hours after classification as major and no
  later than 24 hours after awareness of the incident.

The Commission's DORA Level-2 page maintains the set of implementing/delegated acts.

### Classification

**MULTI_OWNER_STATE_REQUIRED**

The parent Regulation does not itself contain the concrete four-hour/24-hour deadline. A
separate delegated legal act owns the operative detail.

### Needle mapping after freeze

**NO_EXISTING_CLASS_MATCH is currently the safer interpretation.**

This appears first as ordinary delegated-legislation research: a Level-1 rule delegates
details to a legally binding Level-2 act. Complexity or a multi-document chain alone does not
earn a Needle failure class.

That is an important negative control against defining "multi-owner" so broadly that all EU
secondary legislation becomes Needle-shaped.

### Sources

- Commission, DORA implementing and delegated acts:
  https://finance.ec.europa.eu/regulation-and-supervision/financial-services-legislation/implementing-and-delegated-acts/digital-operational-resilience-regulation_en
- EUR-Lex, Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/301, Article 5:
  https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32025R0301

## Case 4 — EUDR actor/product application state

### Practical question

On 26 September 2026, is a large operator already subject to the main EUDR due-diligence
obligations, and do products newly added by the July 2026 delegated amendment follow the same
application date?

### Minimum authoritative chain

- current consolidated Regulation (EU) 2023/1115 after the December 2025 amendment;
- Article 38 now makes the main obligations applicable from **30 December 2026** for
  non-micro/small operators and **30 June 2027** for most micro/small operators;
- Regulation (EU) 2026/2102, published 17 September 2026 and in force, changes Annex I product
  scope;
- the Commission implementation page states that newly added products are subject only from
  **30 December 2027**;
- Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1565 separately changes operation of the EUDR
  Information System.

### Classification

**MULTI_OWNER_STATE_REQUIRED**

For a generic existing product, current consolidated parent text may answer the actor-date
question. But the frozen question also asks about newly added product scope. That requires the
separate 2026 delegated act and its deferred application treatment.

Actor size, prior EUTR coverage and product inclusion can therefore carry separate
application states.

### Needle mapping after freeze

Existing **COHORTED_TRANSITIONAL_APPLICABILITY** and **STATUS_APPLICATION_SEPARATION** are
plausible fits. No new class is proposed.

## Case 5 — CBAM definitive-regime calculation state

### Practical question

For a 2026 importer above the relevant de-minimis threshold, can the operative CBAM
calculation state be reconstructed from Regulation (EU) 2023/956 alone?

### Minimum authoritative chain

No.

The current state includes at least:

- parent Regulation (EU) 2023/956;
- Regulation (EU) 2025/2083, which introduced the single 50-tonne mass threshold for the
  covered mass-based sectors;
- Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2621 for default values;
- corrective Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1740, which repaired missing/incorrect
  production-route indicators, values and codes and applies retroactively from 1 January
  2026;
- separate Commission-published certificate prices: quarterly in 2026, weekly from 2027.

On 26 September 2026 the published 2026 prices are Q1 EUR 75.36 and Q2 EUR 75.28; the Q3 price
is scheduled for publication on 5 October 2026.

### Classification

**MULTI_OWNER_STATE_REQUIRED**

The operative answer depends on amended scope/threshold state, binding implementing values
and a periodically published authoritative price. Parent text or ordinary redline history
does not by itself own the calculation inputs.

The July 2026 correction is particularly consequential: the correction says errors in the
earlier implementing tables made some calculations impossible or wrong and applies the
repair retroactively from 1 January 2026.

### Needle mapping after freeze

Existing **AUTHORITATIVE_EXTERNAL_INPUT_TRIGGER** is a strong fit for the periodically
published price/value inputs; the correction also engages source/version state.

Again this is not fresh validation and does not demonstrate that Needle improves the
research workflow.

## Case 6 — gas-appliance harmonised-standard legal status

### Practical question

On 26 September 2026, does compliance with EN 497:2022 itself confer the EU Gas Appliances
Regulation presumption of conformity?

### Minimum authoritative chain

No.

A European standard's technical existence is separate from its EU legal citation state.

Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1750 records the formal objection and concludes
that EN 497:2022 fails specified essential requirements of Regulation (EU) 2016/426. Article
1 expressly decides **not to publish its reference in the Official Journal**.

The Commission's harmonised-standards pages also warn that their consolidated summary lists
are for information only and do not themselves generate legal effects.

### Classification

**MULTI_OWNER_STATE_REQUIRED**

The legal effect is owned by the EU OJ citation/Commission-decision state, not by the
existence, date or technical validity of the EN standard alone.

This is a clean example of an external/private technical artifact whose legally relevant
status is independently owned by public EU law.

### Needle mapping after freeze

Existing **DYNAMIC_REFERENCE_STATUS** and **PRIVATE_ORIGIN_LEGAL_RECOGNITION** are plausible
owners. No new class is proposed.

## What survives the red team

### 1. There is a real research object beyond text versioning

The surviving distinction is not merely "EU law is complicated".

Across the tranche, the decisive answer can be owned by:

- a national commencement act;
- a delegated/implementing act;
- a product/cohort-specific application rule;
- a corrective implementing act;
- a periodically published authoritative value;
- an OJ citation/non-citation decision.

Those objects can move independently of the parent act's wording.

### 2. Multi-owner state is not itself a Needle mechanism

DORA is the important negative control.

Reading a delegated RTS because Level 1 delegated the deadline to Level 2 is ordinary EU-law
research. If "multi-owner legal state" includes every parent/secondary-act relationship, the
concept is too broad to be useful.

A stronger Phase 2 definition therefore needs a **failure boundary** such as:

> an operative answer depends on an independently moving state whose change is not safely
> reducible to ordinary parent-text amendment/version tracking and whose omission can change
> the legal answer.

### 3. Commercial differentiation remains unproven

Current commercial products already claim substantial parts of this territory:

- CUBE RegPlatform: regulatory change -> obligations -> controls -> traceable evidence;
- Bloomberg Regology: continuously updated law universe, causal citations, applicability and
  obligation/control mapping;
- Thomson Reuters Regulatory Intelligence: applicability, change tracking, obligations and
  control mapping;
- vLex: in-force status, historical versions and amendment comparison.

Public documentation does **not** prove whether these products preserve the specific
multi-owner state transitions seen above.

Therefore the gap is not:

> "nobody tracks EU law / obligations / applicability."

That claim is false.

The unresolved comparator question is narrower:

> when the operative answer changes without a simple parent-text mutation, do strong
> regulatory-intelligence systems preserve the independent owner, effective boundary and
> evidence chain well enough to reconstruct the answer without expert reopening?

That is eligible for investigation; absence from public marketing is not evidence of a
product deficiency.

## Phase 2 gate

Phase 1 passes the gate for a larger outside-in study, but the next study must be stricter.

The candidate proposition is:

> **Some consequential EU-law application questions depend on independently moving legal
> states that are not losslessly represented by current parent text plus ordinary amendment
> history.**

Phase 2 should test prevalence and boundary, not Needle advantage.

Recommended design:

1. mechanically/externally select 20–30 current EU-law application questions across domains;
2. freeze questions before source reconstruction;
3. allow strongest boring official-source research;
4. classify source/state ownership without consulting Needle class labels;
5. require a concrete answer-changing omission before calling a case residual;
6. include obvious text-only controls;
7. only after classification map residual cases to the frozen 26 classes;
8. record **NO_EXISTING_CLASS_MATCH** freely;
9. separately test whether mature commercial systems publicly demonstrate an equivalent
   state contract; where private capability is unknowable, mark it unknown rather than absent.

## Phase 1 disposition

# **EU_LEGAL_STATE_SIGNAL**

This means only:

> the externally selected quick tranche contains enough non-trivial multi-owner operative
> state to justify a better prevalence/boundary study.

It does **not** mean:

- Needle has commercial advantage;
- Needle improves legal research;
- the 26-class taxonomy is representative;
- current regulatory-intelligence products miss these states;
- a new product, schema, corpus release or class is earned.
