# Evidence-driven contractions and identity changes

Status: **HISTORICAL EVIDENCE MAP**

Needle's history is best understood as repeated attempts to make the project smaller without
discarding verified capability.

## 1. Public product -> research/audit engine

### Early product question

Can a live evidence-linked legal-change system provide materially better public understanding than
EUR-Lex/official sources + mature tools + ordinary search + a capable source-grounded model?

### #49 — Evidence / Why this is shown

Across difficult real cases, the evidence presentation did not repeatedly establish a general
public-feed advantage. The project observed parity in several cases and plausible advantage mainly
where dependency effects or evidence closure were otherwise easy to miss.

Result: **REDIRECT** rather than building a larger feed/product shell.

### #59 — Thread comparative value

Thread's Regulation 794/2004 reconstruction was technically strong.

Observed:

- ordinary positive chronology: mostly baseline parity;
- unchanged-text dependency ripple: plausible/systematic audit advantage;
- reviewed corrigendum non-impact: useful audit closure;
- unresolved identity and time distinctions: machine-safety advantage.

But almost all substantive legal facts remained recoverable by a careful baseline.

Verdict:

> **STOP PUBLIC-PRODUCT EXPANSION**

Surviving identity at that point:

> evidence-first legal-change research/audit engine.

Important: Thread/X-Ray/Source Anomaly/temporal/provenance were retained as tools. Their **product
pillar status** stopped; the technical objects did not become false.

## 2. Full Needle -> Method + Core

### #86 — competing identities gate

Five unseen cases compared:

- strong boring baseline;
- Needle Method;
- Needle Core;
- Full Needle.

Key recorded results:

- baseline: 5/5 correct conclusions and 5/5 main traps caught;
- persistent Needle machinery required for raw correctness: 0/5;
- Method changed 0 conclusions but added reusable source/authority/time/uncertainty discipline;
- Core materially helped some persistent historical/repeated-query cases;
- Full Needle added 0/5 material gains over Core.

Disposition:

> **REDIRECT + SIMPLIFY**

Identity became:

> **Needle Method + Needle Core**

Thread/Retrieval/X-Ray/Source Anomaly/Half-Life/feed remained preserved experiments.

## 3. Method + Core -> Method + corpus/protocol

### #87 — Needle Relay

Question: does Method/Core preserve legal understanding across investigator/context loss better than
competent ordinary notes?

Across five sealed cases / three arms:

- 15/15 Stage-B conclusions correct;
- no raw correctness advantage;
- Method had lowest observed reconstruction burden in the pilot and no confirmation disadvantage;
- no Core-over-Method handoff win;
- no parked Full-Needle integration re-entered.

Kill rule fired.

Identity became:

> **Needle Method + corpus/protocol**

Core remained optional/case-earned only when persistence demonstrably prevents a real error or
materially reduces reopening/rediscovery/repeated-query cost.

## 4. Method correctness value narrows further

### #88 / #95

#88 initially looked like adversarial proof, but #95 audited exact prompts and found every question
surfaced or strongly cued the decisive trap.

So #88 proved:

> parity on **SURFACED_TRAP_ADJUDICATION**

not latent detection.

This is a major methodological success: the project corrected its own construct rather than keep
the stronger claim.

### #97 — latent detection

Four fresh adversarial R/M pairs under ordinary prompts:

> **4/4 R-pass / M-pass**

No Method rescues. Method-specific latent-detection advantage was rejected in that bounded setup.

## 5. Corpus/reference identity survives while workflow superiority fails

### #150

Needle's evaluation-integrity discipline transferred once to an external benchmark audit and found
a real contract/provenance issue, but this did not prove broad distinctiveness/product value.

### #203

On a real public benchmark conflict, ordinary QA and corpus-assisted QA converged on the same
consequential disposition. Corpus contribution did not change the decision.

### #210/#212/#214

This was the strongest sealed test of corpus-assisted latent diagnosis.

Recorded result:

> **0 diagnostic rescues / 0 correctness-only C advantages / 0 consequential C-only regressions**

The frozen rule rejected general latent corpus-workflow value for project allocation.

#375 later corrected the evidentiary wording: #214 is a **strong bounded negative with limited
repository reproducibility**, because exact evaluator-package plaintext/raw outputs were not
preserved and one-run/single-grading does not decompose variance.

This is a key historical lesson:

> The corpus may remain valuable as exposed regression/reference material even when supplying it to
> a capable researcher does not improve latent correctness.

## 6. Corpus expansion and scientific identity

After #214, the project did not simply stop. Sponsor continuity reopened scientific discovery.

From #218 onward the corpus expanded under stronger controls:

- strong incumbent/doctrine baseline;
- existing-class first refusal;
- null hypothesis of no new class;
- explicit negative controls;
- anti-growth/compression audits;
- WIP=1;
- no automatic product/schema implication.

New mechanisms included machine compliance artifacts, procedural silence, transitional cohorts,
cross-order incorporation, Member-State options/divergence, territorial regimes, primacy
disapplication, Union-nexus applicability, choice of law, Directive invocability, passport
activation, choice of forum and others.

Important counter-pressure:

- #244 merged three class pairs;
- many candidate families were rejected;
- #260/#267/#277 attempted further compression and preserved null compression results;
- #278–#280 added no new classes.

## 7. #282 retrospective — research method correction

#282 found no fatal primary-source defect and no forced taxonomy compression.

It **did** find two governance/method problems:

1. mature doctrine was sometimes used too quickly as a proxy for 'no corpus value';
2. a depth-mode decision had been silently superseded by later breadth work.

Canonical repair:

> **Existing is not useless. Different is not valuable. Novel is not demanded.**

A candidate should be rejected only when the incumbent plus existing classes losslessly express the
pre-specified consequential regression distinction. Familiar doctrine can still support useful
regression representation; unfamiliarity alone does not create value.

## 8. #288 governance compression

The project had accumulated many workflows, schemas, docs and overlapping state owners.

#288 clarified:

- `BACKLOG.md` alone owns live mode/WIP;
- modes include DISCOVER / CONSOLIDATE / USE / REVIEW-RELEASE / MAINTAIN;
- historical machinery is passive unless re-earned;
- WIP=1 prevents concurrency, not serial busywork;
- constitutional principles remain, but old architecture is not automatically current.

This was later refined again: evidence-gated completion is valid when no task is earned.

## 9. Frozen 81/26 + Reference Pack

By 25 Sep the scientific corpus froze at:

> **81 cases / 26 classes**

All cases exposed and `REGRESSION_ONLY`.

Reference Pack v0.1 made it externally navigable. Later audits clarified:

- not all corpus records are turnkey executable benchmark tasks;
- the safer identity is **known-failure reference corpus**;
- executable regression status is fixture/case-specific;
- negative/null evidence must remain discoverable to prevent claim resurrection.

## 12. Commercial/job separation

#373 and the subsequent review made a critical conceptual separation:

2. **Needle-specific value can still be unproven**;

## 13. #375 — external evaluation job is real

Public evidence established a recurring paid legal-AI evaluation/retesting/maintenance job.

Needle's surviving commercial hypothesis became DELIVERY, not METHOD:

> Can a Needle-derived workflow reach an evaluator's accepted quality with materially lower
> qualified-expert effort, rework, maintenance burden or turnaround than the evaluator's actual
> alternative?

#375 also improved the evaluation-integrity contract itself and corrected historical evidence
weights (#214, #362).

## 14. EU-specific research and evaluation-science research

Later research deliberately split:

- general legal-evaluation estate validity / representation / maintenance;
- EU-specific operative legal-state reconstruction.

Important EU result:

- cross-owner legal-state composition is real;
- official EU infrastructure is rich;
- broad prevalence of an uncovered gap was not established;
- no basis for calling an ordinary EU legislation tracker a Needle opportunity;
- EU operative-state reconstruction remains a plausible narrower high-consequence vertical.

Important evaluation result:

- representation itself matters;
- valid alternatives, false rejection, non-compensatory errors, versioned task contracts and
  judge uncertainty are first-class evaluation-integrity concerns;
- strong incumbents already implement much of this.

## 15. #400/#402 — first MVP candidate, then hard contraction

#400 chose legal evaluation/oracle maintenance DELIVERY as first MVP candidate while explicitly
preserving Candidate B (EU operative state) and Candidate C (postmortem -> regression/oracle).

The first recommendation jumped too quickly toward outreach.

Sponsor correction required:

> select -> consolidate -> extract core -> harden -> internal replay/red-team -> pre-partner readiness -> external validation

#402 then performed incumbent subtraction.

Result:

- task definition, criteria, legal cutoffs, valid alternatives, all-pass semantics, human
  adjudication and versioning were already mature incumbent hygiene;
- the large static packet was rejected;
- only a small **Maintenance Delta** residual survived.

Current candidate core:

> change -> evidence/time owner -> affected score-bearing contract -> revised state -> prior-result consequence -> adjudication state

Strongest remaining red-team:

> Candidate A may optimize the cheap propagation/documentation step while expensive legal
> detection/interpretation/adjudication dominates real cost.

That is now an explicit external kill path rather than something to hide.

## 16. #406/#407 — historical contraction re-audited against longitudinal operations

After the historical-memory reconstruction, the sponsor challenged whether the earlier contraction
had over-weighted one-shot legal-answer comparisons and under-tested the workload the old machinery
was originally built for.

#406 plus an independent Astra review concluded:

> **HISTORICAL_CONTRACTION_STILL_WELL_SUPPORTED**

while preserving a narrower unresolved construct:

> longitudinal operational value across sequential source updates, persistent state and repeated
> resource encounters.

The sponsor authorised one bounded retrospective probe (#407).

### #407 frozen workload

Seven operational state-promotion cycles were mechanically selected by chronology after the old
product checkpoint.

Across them:

- 67,911 new official feed events;
- 5,877 root groups;
- 264 eligible maintained-resource observations / 217 unique roots;
- 135 metadata-only observations;
- 99 content-changed observations;
- 30 unresolved source observations;
- 42 repeated eligible roots.

A strong lightweight comparator was allowed the ordinary monitoring primitives:

- root/work grouping;
- prior/current source snapshots;
- content and metadata hashes;
- maintained notes/state;
- ordinary legal review when content actually changed.

Using only those fields, the comparator reproduced Needle's source-change classification:

> **264 / 264**

with zero mismatches.

For repeated resources, all 43 transitions with known content state preserved exact hash-chain
continuity that the same lightweight ledger could maintain directly.

Most importantly, the historical Needle treatment attempted legal analysis on all 99 content-change
observations but established:

> **0 legal candidates / 0 legal outcomes / 0 CHANGE_FEED items**.

Result:

> **LIGHTWEIGHT_BASELINE_SUFFICIENT**

This does not erase the known successful 2026/2104 operational slice or deny that Needle built a
working source monitor. It means the richer persistent legal-state Core did not show incremental
operational value over a much smaller competent monitoring/state ledger on the chronology-selected
workload.

Historical operational Core therefore remains parked.

Candidate-B EU operative-state work may still reuse narrow historical components when a real
practitioner-owned job earns them; #407 specifically argues against reviving the generic monitoring
stack merely because it exists.

## Bottom line

Needle did not 'lose everything'.

It learned, repeatedly, that:

- technical correctness is not user value;
- useful structure is not automatically a product;
- a capable baseline deserves full credit;
- machinery can remain valid while its default/product role is rejected;
- negative experiments should shrink claims, not erase historical capability;
- the corpus/reference/evaluation discipline is the part that repeatedly survived.
