### F2 — Commission EUDAMED overview
https://health.ec.europa.eu/medical-devices-eudamed/overview_en

  - Actor registration;

### F3 — Decision (EU) 2025/2371
https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec/2025/2371/oj/eng

Material fact used:
- the Decision declared the first four EUDAMED electronic systems functional, triggering the transition mechanics that led to mandatory use from 28 May 2026 under the amended MDR/IVDR framework.

### F4 — Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/977
https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2026/977/oj/eng

Material fact used:

### F5 — Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/1359
https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2026/1359/oj/eng

Material fact used:

### F6 — Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/1451
https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2026/1451/oj/eng

Material fact used:
- it amends MDR regarding the list of implantable and class III devices exempted from the obligation to perform clinical investigations.

### F7 — COM(2025) 1023 procedure 2025/0404(COD)
https://eur-lex.europa.eu/procedure/EN/2025_404?sortOrder=asc

Material fact used:

## Editorial changes authorised

### R1 — distinguish landmark dates from current operation

Keep:
- MDR general application: 26 May 2021;
- IVDR general application: 26 May 2022;
- transition qualification.

Add:
- 28 May 2026 as a **current operational EUDAMED milestone**, not a new regime-wide application date.

Qualification:
- do not say all EUDAMED modules are mandatory;
- do not infer a device-specific compliance outcome from the milestone.

### R2 — remove local completeness implication

Replace:
> What changed after the core Regulations?

with an explicitly bounded later-developments heading and visible statement that the landing page does not enumerate every later act.

### R3 — minimum later-act context

Keep Regulation (EU) 2024/1860 as a key enacted change.

Add only enough current examples to defeat the implication that 2024/1860 is the latest/only later enacted development:
- Decision (EU) 2025/2371;
- Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/977;
- Delegated Regulations (EU) 2026/1359 and 2026/1451.

Do not reproduce the full Commission act list. Route deeper act-family exploration to `/regime-v2/` and the official overview.

### R4 — proposal freshness

Link COM(2025) 1023 to the current EUR-Lex procedure and describe it as an ongoing proposal at the evidence checkpoint.

## Falsifier retained

If honest current-state orientation requires reproducing a broad act inventory, the public regime page should narrow or park rather than becoming a second relationship browser.
