# Issue #321 — frozen class-support independence audit

Date: 2026-09-25  
Disposition: **INDEPENDENCE_ADEQUATE**

## Question

Do the 3–5 accepted cases behind each frozen trap class provide genuinely independent
generality evidence, or does raw case count materially overstate support through
same-chain replication, composition cases or one evaluation suite?

## Method

The audit separates four different notions that raw case count can blur:

- **ORTHOGONAL_GENERALITY** — materially distinct legal instruments/domains/source chains
  instantiate the same causal owner;
- **SAME_CHAIN_REPLICATION** — multiple cases were admitted together from one research
  issue or closely related mechanism;
- **COMPOSITION_SUPPORT** — a case proves coexistence/anti-merge but is not counted as a
  standalone single-class positive;
- **EVALUATION_SUPPORT** — an exposed case came through #88 or #97.

No new legal research or model execution was performed.

## Mechanical profile

Across the frozen 81/26 corpus:

- every class has **at least 2 single-class positive cases** after multi-class composition
  cases are removed;
- 18/26 classes contain at least one same-provenance-issue sibling pair;
- 8/26 classes have EVALUATION support;
- 5/26 classes have explicit multi-class composition support;
- the 10 EVALUATION cases still come from only two programmes (#88 and #97).

Same-issue provenance is therefore common, but it is not itself proof of dependence:
many discovery issues deliberately selected two orthogonal positives before admitting a
class.

## Classes with the thinnest single-class provenance

Five classes reduce to exactly **two single-class positives from one provenance issue**
after composition cases are stripped out:

| Class | Pure positives | Additional support | Interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `CHOICE_OF_FORUM_STATE` | Brussels I + Succession, both #276 | matrimonial-property composition #279 | Two distinct EU jurisdiction regimes survive; composition proves forum choice remains separate from law choice/participation. Narrow-domain but real generality. |
| `CHOICE_OF_LAW_STATE` | Rome I + Succession, both #264 | matrimonial-property composition #279 | Bilateral contract choice and unilateral succession professio juris are materially different mechanisms under one applicable-law owner; composition provides anti-merge support. |
| `COHORTED_TRANSITIONAL_APPLICABILITY` | AI Act + MDR, both #239 | DPF #247 and MiCA #250 are multi-class positives | The pure pair is same-issue, but the full accepted support spans AI, medical devices, international data transfers and crypto-assets across three issues. |
| `DIRECTIVE_INVOCABILITY_STATE` | Marshall + Farrell, both #266 | K.L. v X composition #274 | Public-authority vertical reliance and private-law emanation status are materially distinct defendant settings; K.L. independently supplies the horizontal/Charter anti-merge boundary. |
| `EU_PRIMACY_DISAPPLICATION_STATE` | RFC Seraing + Petlichev, both #259 | K.L. v X composition #274 | Sports/effective-protection and criminal/procedural conflicts are distinct settings; K.L. independently shows why disapplication cannot be collapsed into Directive invocability. |

None of these collapses to a one-example class after composition is removed.

## Same-chain replication is not automatically weak evidence

Eighteen classes contain at least one pair admitted in the same issue.

Examples show why provenance issue count is only a warning signal:

- #270 deliberately paired MiFID investment-services passporting with the European
  Professional Card to test the same cross-border activation owner outside finance;
- #241 paired merger-control and medicinal-product clock suspension before #256 later
  added food-safety generality;
- #238 paired merger deemed compatibility with Staff Regulations deemed no-objection,
  already different administrative settings;
- #263 paired GDPR targeting with MAR regulated-instrument nexus before #278 added the AI
  output-use setting;
- #255 paired French and Danish territorial regimes before #257 added Cyprus Protocol 10.

The research question in these issues was often exactly whether one causal owner survived
across orthogonal examples. Treating "same issue" as "same evidence" would erase that
design.

## Composition support

Five classes have direct composition/anti-merge evidence:

- `CHOICE_OF_FORUM_STATE`;
- `CHOICE_OF_LAW_STATE`;
- `DIFFERENTIATED_MEMBER_STATE_PARTICIPATION`;
- `DIRECTIVE_INVOCABILITY_STATE`;
- `EU_PRIMACY_DISAPPLICATION_STATE`.

The important composition cases are not counted as independent experiments. Their value is
different: they prove that the relevant state owners can coexist in one legal problem and
cannot be merged losslessly.

That evidence strengthens taxonomy independence, not population generality.

## Strongest support profiles

Several classes have unusually dispersed support even before semantic review:

- `DYNAMIC_REFERENCE_STATUS`: 5 cases / 5 provenance issues / 5 stored domains;
- `AUTHORITATIVE_EXTERNAL_INPUT_TRIGGER`: 4 / 4 / 4;
- `JUDICIAL_VALIDITY_TEXT_DIVERGENCE`: 4 / 4 / 4;
- `VERTICAL_SPATIAL_EXTENT`: 3 / 3 / 3;
- `SUBDAY_TEMPORAL_BOUNDARY`: 3 / 3 / 3;
- `JUDICIAL_INTERPRETATION_TEMPORAL_EFFECT`: 3 / 3 / 3.

These are not statistical samples, but raw support count is least misleading for these
families.

## Narrow-domain support that remains adequate

Some classes are intentionally doctrine-specific:

- `PARALLEL_INSTRUMENT_LIFECYCLE` — three distinct modernised-agreement structures
  (Mercosur, Mexico, Chile), one of which entered through latent evaluation;
- `CHOICE_OF_FORUM_STATE` and `CHOICE_OF_LAW_STATE` — private-international-law state
  owners across distinct EU instruments;
- `JUDICIAL_INTERPRETATION_TEMPORAL_EFFECT` — one CJEU temporal-effects doctrine tested
  across materially different substantive fields.

A class does not need cross-domain breadth beyond the scope its definition actually
claims. Requiring unrelated doctrine merely to make the support look diverse would reward
taxonomy overbreadth.

## The important remaining limitation

**Generality support is not experimental independence.**

The frozen corpus still has:

- 71 DERIVATION cases;
- 10 EVALUATION cases;
- all EVALUATION cases from #88 and #97;
- only 8/26 classes with any EVALUATION case.

Therefore:

> multiple orthogonal derivation examples support that a failure family is reusable;
> they do not establish prevalence, benchmark representativeness or comparative model
> performance.

#309 remains the controlling limitation for those claims.

## Deferred residual-value question

#286 deliberately preselected `CHOICE_OF_FORUM_STATE` for a later depth stress test
because:

- it is among the newest classes;
- all three cases remain within private international law;
- one case is composition evidence;
- mature incumbent jurisdiction-agreement doctrine already owns the law.

#288 deferred that run for governance compression; it did not scientifically reject it.

#321 does **not** answer the separate question:

> does preserving `CHOICE_OF_FORUM_STATE` as a corpus class add a consequential
> regression distinction beyond simply applying competent jurisdiction-agreement doctrine?

Its independence is adequate for the class's current narrow definition, but its residual
corpus-specific value remains a legitimate bounded question.

## Disposition

**INDEPENDENCE_ADEQUATE**

No frozen class materially collapses to a single unsupported positive once same-chain and
composition structure are examined.

No new maintained independence registry is warranted.

Durable interpretation:

- 3–5 cases/class is a support count, not an independence score;
- same-issue sibling cases must be read through their selection design;
- composition cases support anti-merge structure, not independent population evidence;
- all 26 classes currently have sufficient distinct positive settings for their stated
  regression/reference role;
- #309's evaluation-independence limitation remains fully intact.

No corpus, taxonomy or Reference Pack change follows.
