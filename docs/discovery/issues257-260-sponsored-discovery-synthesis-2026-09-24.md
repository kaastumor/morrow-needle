## #257 — Cyprus sub-state territorial generality

Protocol No 10 suspends the acquis in areas of the Republic of Cyprus where the
Government does not exercise effective control, while Cyprus remains one EU
Member State.

The Green Line remains a special interface regime rather than an external EU
border.

This validates `SUBSTATE_TERRITORIAL_REGIME` beyond overseas/OCT
configurations.

One Cyprus DERIVATION case was added.

## #259 — EU primacy disapplication state

New class:

`EU_PRIMACY_DISAPPLICATION_STATE`

Definition:

> A national provision can remain formally valid, in force and textually
> current while directly effective EU law requires it to be disapplied in the
> relevant dispute or scope.

Two orthogonal DERIVATION cases were added:
- Royal Football Club Seraing;
- Petlichev.

Popławski supplies the negative boundary: primacy alone does not require
disapplication where the conflicting EU provision lacks direct effect.

The class remains separate from `JUDICIAL_VALIDITY_TEXT_DIVERGENCE` because
disapplication does not invalidate the national provision.
