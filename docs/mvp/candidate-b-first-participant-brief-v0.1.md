## Purpose

This session tests a narrow workflow question:

> When you already know which standard and EU regime matter, is it easier and safer to determine the operative Official Journal reference status from the Candidate-B card than from the strongest free official-source workflow?

This is not a sales call and not a test of your legal knowledge.

It is also not a full product-conformity exercise.

## What you may use

For official-source tasks you may use:

- the European Commission harmonised-standards / formal-objection navigation;
- EUR-Lex and Official Journal material;
- ordinary browser/search;
- your normal notes/bookmarks;
- a source-grounded general-purpose model if that is genuinely part of your normal workflow.

For Candidate-B tasks you may use:

- the Candidate-B status card;
- every official source linked from the card.

Do not use paid standards/compliance products during this pilot.

## Answer format

For each standard, answer these six questions as of **26 September 2026**:

1. Is the OJ reference **cited**, **cited with restriction**, or **not cited**?
2. What presumption-of-conformity consequence follows from that status?
3. What restriction or scope matters, if any?
4. What legal event owns the current state?
5. Is a future transition already legally fixed?
6. What should **not** be inferred from the status?

Please say when you feel confident enough to stop researching.

You may revise an answer before you say you are finished.

## After the tasks

You will be asked:

- Which workflow would you choose for the next similar task?
- What information was missing?
- What felt redundant?
- What would make the compact card unsafe to rely on?
- Is known-standard status useful on its own?
- Would you instead need the system to discover which standards apply to a product?

## Safety boundary

The Candidate-B card does **not** determine full product conformity.

It does not supply:

- the standard text;
- technical-requirement interpretation;
- applicable-legislation discovery;
- laboratory/testing evidence;
- notified-body decisions;
- supplier-evidence validation;
- CE-marking advice;
- live updates.

The prototype is frozen historical/evidentiary material for this usability test.
