## Job A — vendor-side independent evaluation / retesting

### Job owner / buyer

Legal-AI vendors that want independent evidence of product performance.

Public evidence:

- Legal Benchmarks sells a public application assessment for **$1,000**, private evaluation
  at custom pricing, and quarterly performance certification:
  https://www.legalbenchmarks.ai/benchmarks
- HAQQ states that it handed its product to an independent third-party evaluator and intends
  to keep participating in future rounds:
  https://www.haqq.ai/blog/legal-ai-benchmark-2026

This establishes more than category existence:
- an identifiable buyer type;
- a paid evaluation offering;
- an actual vendor using independent evaluation;
- a repeated/retest cycle.

### Decision changed

The work can change:
- public performance claims;
- certification;
- product improvement priorities;
- confidence in release/version quality;
- comparative market evidence.

### Current strongest solution

Give the incumbent full credit:

- real practitioner-contributed tasks;
- private task instructions/reference files/answer keys;
- lawyer-authored binary criteria;
- repeat execution;
- multiple automated judges;
- disagreement escalation to qualified lawyers;
- confidential failure-mode diagnostics;
- private/public disclosure controls.

Sources:
- https://www.legalbenchmarks.ai/methodology
- https://www.legalbenchmarks.ai/leaderboard/applications

### Acceptance criteria

Current practice already has a strong contract:

- task passes only when all applicable substantive criteria pass;
- criteria are fixed before testing;
- repeated attempts reduce lucky-pass effects;
- material judge disagreements escalate to legal review;
- vendor-facing reports preserve task-level/failure diagnostics.

This is not an underdeveloped baseline.

### Externally evidenced burden

The strongest residual evidence comes from benchmark production itself.

Vals' legal-AI consortium report records that:

- consortium firms supplied questions, source documents, ideal responses and correctness
  criteria;
- Vals estimated that **fully manual** scoring of all outputs would have required more than
  **400 hours** for one person, while its actual process already used automated evaluation;
- firms were constrained in their capacity to provide detailed/complex reference answers;
- prior work product was difficult to reuse because of confidentiality/ethical constraints;
- the report explicitly says richer benchmark tasks were limited by appropriate data and
  contributor capacity.

These facts establish production difficulty and scarce-expert dependence. They do **not**
by themselves establish an unmet purchasable gap or that Needle would reduce total delivery
cost.

Source:
https://www.vals.ai/industry-reports/vlair-2-27-25

This is a real production bottleneck, not a speculative Needle failure class.

Current Legal Benchmarks still recruits practitioners to:
- contribute benchmark tasks;
- review methodology;
- judge model answers.

Source:
https://www.legalbenchmarks.ai/community

This supports continuing dependence on scarce expert input, although it does **not** prove
that current benchmark providers want to outsource or buy a Needle-derived service.

### Possible Needle residual

The only residual worth testing is:

> **DELIVERY:** can a Needle-derived evaluation/oracle workflow reduce the qualified-lawyer
> time, rework or adjudication burden needed to produce an accepted legal benchmark oracle
> without increasing legal error?

Possible contributions could include:
- disciplined source/version capture;
- explicit opposite-error boundaries;
- reusable oracle/PASS-FAIL contracts;
- structured evidence closure;
- preservation of uncertainty/adjudication state.

These are candidate delivery aids.

The 26 class labels themselves are **not** the value claim.

### Why this does not rescue #214/#327/#362

- #214 concerns giving Needle material to a research system/user to improve latent diagnosis.
  That is not this job.
- #327 concerns generic legal-research companion use.
  That is not this job.
- #362 gave excellent ordinary evaluator practice full credit and found no Needle discipline
  or artifact delta on its selected oracle-maintenance pair.

#362 therefore supplies directional counterevidence to an intrinsic **better-oracle** story,
but its #375 audit limitations mean it should not be described as universally blocking that
possibility by itself.

More importantly, no current evidence positively establishes an intrinsic better-oracle
claim. The downstream DELIVERY question is different: whether an already accepted oracle can
be produced with less total qualified-expert burden.

The new claim must therefore remain **DELIVERY**, not METHOD.

## Job B — buyer-side procurement / pilot evaluation

### Evidence

Legal Benchmarks' Evaluation Framework was built/validated by more than 100 legal and
technology leaders across 82 organisations and 25 countries.

It describes:
- duplicated buyer evaluation effort;
- inconsistent evaluation;
- decisions driven too heavily by demos/marketing;
- pre-demo, demo and hands-on pilot gates.

Source:
https://www.legalbenchmarks.ai/research/evaluation-framework

The Dutch adoption survey adds a concrete reliability need:
- 67.8% of 115 respondents said the first AI legal-research answer was usable half the time
  or less;
- 72.2% rated outdated, irrelevant or wrong-jurisdiction sources as serious;
- 82.6% rated invented/incorrect law, facts or citations as serious.

Source:
https://www.legalbenchmarks.ai/research/dutch-legal-ai-adoption-survey

### Assessment

This is a real buyer job with explicit decision gates.

However:
- the open Evaluation Framework already supplies a strong procurement method;
- buyer-owned workflow pilots are the correct incumbent;
- no public evidence shows a specific remaining procurement task where Needle improves
  decisions or materially reduces buyer burden.

Disposition:

> **JOB REAL; NEEDLE-SHAPED RESIDUAL NOT YET EVIDENCED**

Do not run a Needle comparison here merely because procurement is painful.

## Why Job A is selected

Job A is the only lane where all of the following are externally visible:

1. a paid external evaluation service exists;
2. legal-AI vendors actually use independent evaluation;
3. retesting/certification makes the activity recurring;
4. objective acceptance rules already exist;
5. expert task/oracle production burden is documented;

That is sufficient to justify one bounded **delivery** test.

It is not sufficient to justify:
- a product;

- corpus expansion;
- proprietary benchmark construction;
- a claim of Needle-specific advantage.

## Required next test boundary

Do **not** start from Needle classes.

The minimum credible pilot requires an external dependency that the current project cannot
self-supply without contaminating the result.

### Required external inputs

1. **independently supplied workload brief**
   - a real or realistic legal-evaluation task owned by an evaluator/vendor/legal team;
   - jurisdiction/scope/use fixed before Needle inspection.

2. **qualified legal reviewer/adjudicator**
   - independently decides whether the resulting oracle/reference answer and criteria meet
     the accepted legal standard;
   - records corrections, disputes and uncertainty.

3. **buyer/evaluator-set acceptance threshold**
   - what counts as accepted deliverable quality;
   - no Needle-authored success definition after the result.

### Claim

Freeze only:

> **DELIVERY:** a Needle-derived workflow can produce an accepted legal evaluation
> oracle/reference-answer package with materially lower total qualified-expert burden than
> the actual alternative.

### Primary measurements

- qualified reviewer minutes/hours;
- number and severity of legal corrections;
- rework rounds;
- unresolved disputes/uncertainties;
- total production turnaround;
- final acceptance / rejection.

Do not count:
- class matches;
- extra metadata;
- longer rubrics;
- prettier structure.

### Kill rule

If the Needle-derived workflow does not reduce the predeclared delivery burden while
preserving accepted legal quality, stop this commercial delivery hypothesis.

Do not convert a null into a METHOD claim.
