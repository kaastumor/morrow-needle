Low-value import customs changes

Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/1022

This act changes definitions, customs-declaration rules and data elements connected to the temporary EUR 3 customs duty on distance sales of imported goods in consignments with an intrinsic value not exceeding EUR 150. It amends an existing delegated customs regulation rather than creating a standalone customs regime.

Scope

What this example actually covers

  • Distance sales of imported goods in consignments with an intrinsic value not exceeding EUR 150 are the stated context for the temporary EUR 3 customs duty addressed by the amendments.
  • The delegated act changes definitions, customs declarations and data elements in Delegated Regulation (EU) 2015/2446.
  • Some specified Annex data elements have their own later mandatory date, while operators may use them voluntarily earlier.

Do not infer from this page: that every low-value parcel owes EUR 3, that the same declaration route applies to every shipment, or that this delegated regulation is the whole customs regime.

Time

Several dates mean different things

  1. 30 Apr 2026
    ADOPTIONDelegated act adopted

    EUR-Lex records 30 April 2026 as the date of the delegated regulation.

  2. 1 Jul 2026
    PUBLICATION + APPLICATIONPublished; general application date stated

    The Official Journal publication is dated 1 July. Article 2 states that the Regulation applies from 1 July 2026.

  3. 2 Jul 2026
    ENTRY INTO FORCEDay after publication

    Article 2 separately states that entry into force occurs on the day following publication. Do not reorder these concepts merely because application is stated one day earlier.

  4. 1 Jul 2026
    OPTIONAL EARLY USESelected Annex data may be provided voluntarily

    Operators may voluntarily provide the data in point (2)(a) and (b) of the Annex from 1 July.

  5. 4 Aug 2026
    LANGUAGE-SCOPED CORRIGENDUMGerman and Dutch corrected

    The later corrigendum expressly does not concern the English version. Language-expression history is therefore not globally identical.

  6. 1 Nov 2026
    ANNEX APPLICATIONSelected Annex data become mandatory

    Point (2)(a) and (b) of the Annex applies from 1 November 2026.

Relationships

This is an amendment inside a wider customs framework

  • AMENDSDelegated Regulation (EU) 2015/2446

    The 2026 act changes the existing delegated customs rules rather than replacing them wholesale.

  • UNDERLYING FRAMEWORKRegulation (EU) No 952/2013 — Union Customs Code

    The amended delegated regulation supplements the Union Customs Code.

  • CORRECTED IN SOME LANGUAGESCorrigendum 2026/90648

    The August corrigendum concerns German and Dutch only and explicitly states that English is unaffected.

These are represented relationships for this example. A navigation edge or amendment link is not itself a conclusion about a particular shipment.

Why this page exists

The medical-device pages use an economic-operator/core-regulation contract. This customs example is intentionally separate: it tests which Needle conventions transfer safely—source/version identity, typed dates, explicit relationships and visible limits—without pretending that medical roles or a single “effective date” apply to every EU instrument.
