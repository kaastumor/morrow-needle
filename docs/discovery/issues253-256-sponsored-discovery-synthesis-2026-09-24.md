## #255 — sub-state territorial regime

New class:

`SUBSTATE_TERRITORIAL_REGIME`

Definition:

> EU-law applicability can differ between territories constitutionally linked to
> the same sovereign Member State because Treaty or instrument-specific
> territorial status attaches to the territory rather than the country label.

Orthogonal derivation cases:
1. France — outermost regions versus OCTs;
2. Denmark — mainland versus Greenland/Faroe territorial/customs scope.

The class is distinct from Member-State participation, linked-order
incorporation and vertical spatial extent.

## #256 — food-law procedural clock suspension

Regulation 2065/2003 Article 8 confirms that EFSA's six-month assessment period
can be suspended while requested supplementary information or explanations are
outstanding.

The rule separately distinguishes extension from suspension.

This generalises `PROCEDURAL_CLOCK_SUSPENSION` beyond merger control and
medicinal-product procedures.

One smoke-flavouring DERIVATION case was added.
