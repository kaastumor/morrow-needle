## Result

# **EU_LEGAL_STATE_BOUNDARY_SUPPORTED — PREVALENCE UNESTABLISHED**

Phase 2 narrows the Phase 1 signal substantially.

Across a frozen 24-question cross-domain stress sample:

- **10/24 — TEXT_SUFFICIENT**
- **2/24 — CHANGE_HISTORY_SUFFICIENT**
- **12/24 — MULTI_OWNER_STATE_REQUIRED**

But **multi-owner** is intentionally not the endpoint.

After a second red-team pass, only **7/24** are classified as a stronger
**INDEPENDENT_OPERATIVE_STATE** residual: omission of a separately moving state can change
the operative answer and the state is not losslessly reducible to ordinary parent-text
amendment/version tracking.

The remaining **5/24** multi-owner cases are normal cross-instrument or national-
implementation research. They are important legal work, but they do not support a distinct
Needle-shaped research category.

This is a **stratified stress sample, not a population-random sample**. Percentages describe
only the frozen sample.

---

## Research question

> On contemporary EU regulatory questions selected before Needle class mapping, when is
> current authoritative text/change history enough, and when does the operative answer
> depend on a separately moving legal state?

Phase 2 tests the narrowed boundary established after Phase 1:

> an operative answer depends on an independently moving state whose change is not safely
> reducible to ordinary parent-text amendment/version tracking and whose omission can change
> the legal answer.

The operator knows Needle's taxonomy. This is therefore not blind discovery and cannot
validate taxonomy coverage independently.

## Strong residuals

### R1 — dynamic authoritative input: CBAM

The parent CBAM regulation does not own all operative 2026 calculation inputs.

The regime uses separately maintained implementing values and Commission-published
certificate prices. The 2026 corrective implementing regulation also repaired missing or
incorrect production-route indicators/values/codes with retroactive application from
1 January 2026.

The key mechanism is not simply an amendment:

> an authoritative numeric/input state can change the compliance calculation while the
> parent obligation remains textually unchanged.

**Post-classification Needle fit:** `AUTHORITATIVE_EXTERNAL_INPUT_TRIGGER`.

No claim is made that Needle is needed to research CBAM.

### R2 — authoritative entity-status state: CER

Directive (EU) 2022/2557 requires each Member State to identify its critical entities by
17 July 2026.

The Directive defines the criteria, but whether a concrete entity has been identified is a
separate national authoritative state.

The operative question therefore is not exhausted by asking whether Article 6 is in force.

**Post-classification Needle fit:** `DYNAMIC_REFERENCE_STATUS` is the closest current
owner, because legal consequences attach to a separately maintained authoritative status
while the parent rule remains unchanged.

### R3 — Member-State-permitted divergence: CSRD FY2025–2026

Directive (EU) 2026/470 amended the CSRD application schedule and expressly permits Member
States to exempt specified undertakings/issuers below the new thresholds from reporting for
financial years beginning in 2025 and 2026.

For an affected undertaking in FY2026, the Union amendment alone therefore does not always
settle actual obligation state.

**Post-classification Needle fit:** `MEMBER_STATE_PERMITTED_DIVERGENCE`.

This is stronger than generic directive transposition because the Union rule itself
authorises bounded national divergence in the operative result.

### R4/R5 — MDR / IVDR transitional eligibility

The amended MDR/IVDR transitions do not operate as simple calendar extensions.

Eligibility can depend on:

- original certificate/declaration state;
- device risk class;
- whether design/intended purpose changed significantly;
- continuing compliance with the earlier regime;
- QMS state;
- a timely formal application;
- a timely written agreement with a notified body.

The historical cohort and private conformity-assessment relationship can therefore determine
whether the device can still lawfully be placed on the market.

**Post-classification Needle fit:** primarily `COHORTED_TRANSITIONAL_APPLICABILITY`;
`PRIVATE_ORIGIN_LEGAL_RECOGNITION` may also be relevant to the notified-body component.

### R6 — Construction Products Regulation product-family migration

Regulation (EU) 2024/3110 is generally applicable, but the old and new CPR regimes coexist.

Commission guidance and Article 95 make migration product-family specific: old harmonised
standards can remain valid under CPR-2011 until withdrawn, while new obligations become
applicable to a product family after the relevant new harmonised technical
specification/implementing act reaches its application boundary.

The operative regime can therefore differ between product families on the same date.

**Post-classification Needle fit:** `DYNAMIC_REFERENCE_STATUS` and
`STATUS_APPLICATION_SEPARATION` are plausible existing owners.

### R7 — harmonised-standard legal status: EN 497:2022

EN 497:2022 can exist as a private European standard while lacking the EU-law status needed
to confer the Gas Appliances Regulation presumption of conformity.

Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/1750 expressly refuses publication of the
standard's reference in the Official Journal after a formal objection.

**Post-classification Needle fit:** `DYNAMIC_REFERENCE_STATUS` plus
`PRIVATE_ORIGIN_LEGAL_RECOGNITION`.

## Important non-residuals

The study would be misleading if it counted every cross-document answer as support.

### DORA

The Level-1 Regulation deliberately delegates exact incident-report timings to Level 2.
Reading the delegated act is ordinary competent legal research.

Result:

> **multi-owner, but not a distinct residual**

### NIS2 national implementation

For a Dutch operative-law question, reading Dutch implementing legislation is ordinary
directive research.

The Commission country-page lag is still a real source-view hazard and confirms that an EU
summary page must not be treated as the national legal truth owner. But the mere need to
read national law does not establish a special legal-state category.

### RED III / Energy Efficiency / Right to Repair

The same anti-inflation rule applies.

A directive requiring national transposition is not a Needle discovery.

Only an additional state that cannot be reduced to ordinary national implementation would
earn the stronger residual classification.

## Phase 1 correction

Phase 1 classified the EUDR question as `MULTI_OWNER_STATE_REQUIRED`.

Phase 2 revises it downward to:

> **CHANGE_HISTORY_SUFFICIENT**

Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/2102 is an amending legal act. EUR-Lex records its
amendment relationship and provision-specific effective dates, including additions that
apply from 30 December 2027.

That is legally intricate, but ordinary amendment/effect tracking can represent it.

Preserving this correction matters: without it, the project would slowly define ordinary
EU amendment law as a special Needle mechanism.

## Official / infrastructure baseline

EU legal-information infrastructure already owns important parts of the problem.

### EUR-Lex / ELI

EUR-Lex and the Publications Office provide:

- stable legal-resource identity;
- document relationships;
- dates/effects;
- consolidated/version views;
- national transposition notifications;
- ELI metadata and machine-readable identifiers.

ELI is explicitly a common model for exchanging **legislation metadata**.

That is substantial infrastructure. Needle must not imply that EU law lacks linked,
structured legal metadata.

But EUR-Lex also states important limits:

- consolidated texts are documentation tools rather than authentic legal acts;
- national-transposition information is supplied under Member-State responsibility and does
  not prejudge Commission verification of completeness/correctness.

The current study's residual is therefore not "EUR-Lex has no metadata".

It is the narrower question of **operative state composition across heterogeneous owners**.

## What the public commercial evidence does **not** establish

Public product descriptions do not show enough detail to determine whether these systems
losslessly represent, as first-class state owners:

- authority/entity designation state;
- private certificate/notified-body transition state;
- OJ citation/non-citation state for standards;
- dynamic official numeric inputs such as a period-specific certificate price;
- bounded national options whose exercise changes one cohort's operative result;
- transition state where different product families migrate between two regulations on
  different events.

But:

> **absence from public product documentation is not evidence of absence from the product.**

Therefore the commercial result is:

# **PUBLIC_CONTRACT_UNCLEAR — NOT GAP_CONFIRMED**

A real product/delivery comparison would require access to product behaviour, documentation
at sufficient depth, a demo, or actual users.

That is separate from the scientific finding that the underlying legal-state structures
exist.

## What this says about Needle

### Supported

The EU-specific research lane is not exhausted.

A narrower research object survives:

> **operative EU legal state can depend on separately moving authoritative, national,
> procedural, cohort, private-recognition or external-input states that are not equivalent
> to the current wording/version of the parent legal act.**

The frozen corpus already contains concepts that describe every strong residual found in
this contaminated stress sample.

### Not supported

This study does **not** establish that:

- the 26 classes are representative of EU-law work;
- 7/24 approximates prevalence in real legal queries;
- Needle discovers these states better than a strong lawyer or LLM;
- Needle's taxonomy is needed to solve them;
- regulatory-intelligence products omit them;
- a legal-state product should be built;
- corpus growth is warranted;
- the Reference Pack creates commercial value.

### Particularly important negative

Several apparently complex regimes were fully handled by current text or ordinary change
history.

The study therefore rejects the stronger formulation:

> "modern EU regulation generally requires a special multi-owner legal-state model."

That claim is too broad.

---

## Research consequence

The correct EU-specific hypothesis is now:

> **Some consequential EU-law application questions depend on independently moving
> operative state that cannot be reduced to current parent text plus ordinary
> amendment/version history.**

That proposition survives.

The unresolved questions are now sharper:

1. **real-task prevalence** — how often does the residual occur in independently sourced
   real EU-law work rather than a stratified stress sample?
2. **operational burden** — when it occurs, does a strong researcher or regulatory-
   intelligence workflow need material reopening/reconstruction work?
3. **representation sufficiency** — can existing official/commercial systems preserve the
   state losslessly without a bespoke Needle layer?
4. **taxonomy independence** — would an independent investigator identify the same state
   distinctions without seeing Needle's 26 classes?
5. **failure consequence** — how often does omitting the separate state actually reverse or
   materially distort an answer rather than merely add detail?

Those are stronger next questions than another class hunt.
