# Issue #392 — research frontier and external evidence gate

Date: 2026-09-26  
Mode: **REVIEW / CONSOLIDATE — RESEARCH FRONTIER**

## Decision

# **CONSOLIDATE_THEN_EXTERNAL_GATE**

The sponsor's challenge to the earlier "ready for external reality, no more internal proof"
assumption was correct.

There **were** material internal/public research questions left.

They produced real project changes:

- the EU-law thesis was separated into existence versus prevalence;
- the evaluation protocol was repaired to declare representation/decision fit before
  rubric-specific checks;
- the false-rejection hypothesis was narrowed by strong practitioner-benchmark
  counterevidence;
- agent trajectories were separated into diagnostic versus legally constitutive paths;
- several apparent commercial gaps were reclassified as occupied, unknown or
  external-access dependent.

After that work, however, the frontier has moved.

The remaining decision-relevant uncertainties now depend predominantly on evidence that
cannot be manufactured credibly from another internally selected public artifact.

The right next state is therefore:

> **consolidate what is now known, then treat external workflow/product/adjudication evidence
> as the next substantive source of truth.**

This is not the same premature conclusion that #377 challenged.

It is the result **after** the additional research programme.

---

## 1. What the post-#375 research actually changed

### EU legal-state research — #377

Supported:

> some EU-law questions genuinely depend on independently moving operative state that cannot
> be reduced to current parent text plus ordinary amendment/cross-instrument interpretation.

Not supported:

> that residual is common across ordinary EU-law research or implementation work.

Evidence:

- stratified structural stress sample: 7/24 strong residuals;
- official implementation-Q&A sample: 0/16;
- mechanically selected CJEU matter-specific contrast: 1/12.

The samples are not pooled because their selection frames differ.

Result:

> **EU_LEGAL_STATE_EXISTENCE_SUPPORTED — PREVALENCE_NOT_SUPPORTED**

The corpus remains appropriately interpreted as adversarial/enriched known-failure memory,
not a frequency map.

### Evaluation representation — #381

Supported:

> representation choice is part of the measurement contract.

Different representations preserve different signals:

- atomic criteria -> mandatory requirements, vetoes, diagnosis;
- comparative judgment -> holistic relative ranking;
- issue trees -> coverage/correctness structure;
- qualified holistic review -> professional acceptability;
- hybrids -> potentially appropriate when roles are frozen.

Result:

> **REPRESENTATION_CHOICE_MATTERS**

The canonical protocol was materially repaired.

### Legal false rejection — #385

Supported:

> incomplete acceptable-set authoring is a credible general rubric failure mechanism.

Not supported:

> expert legal rubrics currently show demonstrated material false-rejection prevalence.

The bounded audit located:

- strong alternative-aware safeguards in DELTA / Legal Benchmarks;
- structural risk in strict/finite rubric systems;
- zero clean public legal positives under the strict acceptance-boundary definition.

Result:

> **PUBLIC_EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT**

### Legal-agent trajectory — #390

Supported:

- legal-agent traces are useful diagnostics;
- some deployed-agent behaviors are legally constitutive.

Not supported:

> Needle's surviving legal-research identity requires a hidden-path trajectory layer.

Harvey LAB shows diagnostic trajectory-performance associations.
Aithos LARA shows legally constitutive agent behavior.
Needle's source/time/authority failures remain primarily final result/evidence states.

Result:

> **TRAJECTORY_IS_DIAGNOSTIC_ONLY_FOR_NEEDLE_SCOPE**

## 2. Remaining lane review

### A. Temporal/oracle maintenance

#### What is already known

#375 established a real public example where score-bearing legal values were stale/wrong.

The current protocol now explicitly owns:

- source/effective period;
- governing legal time;
- event-specific temporal ownership;
- versioned task/evaluation state;
- legal/source drift.

#377 independently reinforced that operative law can depend on state that is not equivalent to
current parent text.

Commercial regulatory-intelligence systems publicly claim substantial:

- continuous legal updating;
- applicability;
- obligation mapping;
- effective-date/change handling;
- audit history.

#### What another public hunt could answer

It could find another stale criterion.

#### What it cannot credibly answer

Without benchmark-maintainer records or a pre-existing unbiased maintenance corpus, it
cannot establish:

- frequency of oracle drift;
- expert hours spent detecting/repairing it;
- mean time to repair;
- downstream score impact;
- whether mature maintainers already catch most drift before publication;
- whether Needle materially reduces maintenance burden.

Another mutable-law benchmark search would increasingly select for the phenomenon it wants
to find.

Disposition:

> **REAL OPERATIONAL PROBLEM; PUBLIC INTERNAL VALUE FRONTIER REACHED**

External evidence needed:

- maintained benchmark/evaluation estate;
- revision history;
- issue/incident log;
- qualified maintenance effort;
- preferably pre/post repair consequences.

Do not open another defect hunt.

### B. Failure ecology / prevalence

#### What is already known

Needle's 81-case corpus is intentionally adversarial.

#377 showed why this distinction matters: enriched structural samples can produce very
different residual rates from externally sourced task populations.

#### Public-source loophole check

DELTA is a valuable external task estate:

- tasks selected from >200 Dutch practice assignments;
- current public release exposes 15 tasks / 273 criteria;
- practitioners scored model outputs.

But the inspectable repository currently exposes the task/criterion contract rather than a
complete mechanically usable raw model-output/failure corpus for independent failure-type
sampling.

Published reports provide benchmark findings, not a neutral raw failure population suitable
for a new Needle taxonomy-prevalence estimate.

Other open legal benchmarks either:

- target different constructs;
- are deliberately hard-set/enriched;
- are historical/classification benchmarks;
- would require the project to generate new outputs itself.

Generating a new model run and then classifying its failures would measure one chosen model /
harness under project-authored conditions, not population prevalence of legal-AI failure.

Disposition:

> **NO CURRENT PUBLIC SAMPLING FRAME THAT JUSTIFIES ANOTHER PREVALENCE PROGRAMME**

External evidence needed:

- independently generated real task/output pairs;
- production incident/postmortem logs;
- an external evaluator's failure estate;
- or a future public corpus that exposes raw outputs under a defensible sampling process.

### C. Commercial product-contract map

#### What is already known

The market has real incumbents across:

- independent legal benchmarking;
- vendor/law-firm internal QA;
- horizontal eval/observability;
- regulatory intelligence/change management;
- general AI assurance.

Public evidence materially killed several naive gaps:

- generic legal benchmarking is occupied;
- regression tooling is occupied;
- generic trajectory observability is occupied;
- legal-content/source access is heavily occupied;
- regulatory monitoring/applicability/obligation work is heavily occupied.

#### Public-evidence limit

A marketing/methodology page can establish a claimed capability.

It generally cannot establish that an implementation **does not** preserve:

- dynamic legal state;
- typed transitions;
- oracle revision history;
- client-specific applicability;
- internal QA;
- specific trajectory rules.

Therefore:

> **not publicly documented != absent**

Continuing public website review now risks manufacturing a gap from non-disclosure.

Disposition:

> **PUBLIC CONTRACT MAP SATURATED**

External evidence needed:

- product demo;
- trial/sandbox access;
- detailed technical/procurement documentation;
- user/operator interviews;
- observed output on a frozen workload.

### D. Customer-specific maintained evaluation estate

This remains a plausible job:

> organisations want evaluation tied to their own work, quality thresholds and changing law.

Large organisations demonstrably build this internally.

But the decisive questions are now commercial/operational:

- what do smaller organisations do today?
- how much qualified effort does it consume?
- what do they distrust about current tools?
- what must stay confidential?
- who owns maintenance?
- would they outsource it?
- what quality threshold is acceptable?
- what would they pay or stop doing?

No public benchmark can answer those questions.

Disposition:

> **JOB PLAUSIBLE — BUYER EVIDENCE REQUIRED**

## 3. Exact external evidence gates

The project should stop using "external validation" as one vague bucket.

There are four different external gates.

### Gate 1 — evaluator DELIVERY test

Question:

> Can a Needle-derived workflow deliver an already acceptable legal evaluation/oracle
> package with materially lower total qualified-expert burden than the evaluator's actual
> alternative?

Requires:

- evaluator-owned workload;
- actual incumbent workflow;
- independent acceptance threshold;
- all expert time;
- correction/rework;
- tool/model/other labor cost;
- turnaround;
- disputes/adjudication.

This is the existing #375 commercial DELIVERY hypothesis.

### Gate 2 — qualified evaluation-science adjudication

Questions:

- does representation choice change valid legal quality decisions?
- do criterion contracts falsely reject defensible alternatives?

Requires:

- qualified legal reviewers independent of the representation;
- same frozen work products;
- preserved disagreement;
- predeclared task families and decision objective.

This is scientific, not necessarily commercial.

### Gate 3 — product-capability access

Question:

> Do mature regulatory-intelligence/eval systems already preserve the specific legal-state /
> maintenance contracts Needle considers interesting?

Requires one or more of:

- product demo;
- real sandbox;
- detailed technical contract;
- frozen test workload;
- practitioner/operator evidence.

Public absence is not a valid negative.

### Gate 4 — real workflow / incident evidence

Questions:

- how prevalent are Needle-type failure structures in actual work?
- when is trajectory constitutive?
- what is oracle-maintenance burden?

Requires:

- real task/output/failure population;
- incident logs;
- workflow policies;
- production traces;
- maintenance history.

This gate is the strongest protection against another internally enriched case hunt.

## 4. What remains internally eligible

External-gate status does **not** mean "do nothing".

The following remain legitimate without reopening discovery:

### Correctness / source-integrity maintenance

Act when:

- a cited source changes/disappears;
- an accepted legal fact is shown wrong;
- a benchmark/task revision changes interpretation;
- a repository reproducibility defect is discovered.

### Consolidation

Allowed when it removes duplicated/stale interpretation without changing claims.

### Release integrity

Reference Pack v0.2 remains current/frozen.

No v0.3 is earned from the research above.

### Responding to genuinely new public evidence

A new external dataset, benchmark release, product contract or independently observed
failure can reopen a bounded question.

The trigger must be **new evidence**, not the desire to keep the experiment conveyor moving.

## 5. What remains parked even after external contact

External access does not revive failed project theses automatically.

Keep parked unless new evidence directly supports them:

- generic corpus-assisted latent correctness/diagnostic superiority;
- generic legal-research companion advantage;
- broad product/platform build;
- proprietary legal-reasoning method;
- claim that the 26 classes are representative;
- taxonomy/corpus growth for coverage aesthetics;
- generic trajectory product;
- generic regulatory-intelligence product;
- open-source/license change.

#214/#327/#366 remain binding against casual resurrection.

## 6. Owner gate

The sponsor previously deferred partner/evaluator outreach.

Nothing in the current conversation explicitly changes that owner-only decision into an
authorisation to contact an external party.

Therefore:

> **the project may prepare the external evidence contract, but must not initiate partner
> outreach until the sponsor explicitly reopens that gate.**

This distinction matters:

- research allocation can conclude external evidence is now needed;
- the assistant does not infer permission to contact partners.

---

## Final disposition

# **CONSOLIDATE_THEN_EXTERNAL_GATE**

The consolidation is this review.

After it is accepted:

> **the next substantive evidence source for value/product discovery is external.**

This conclusion is stronger than the pre-#377 version because the project actually tested
the most promising additional public lanes and preserved their negative/narrowing results.

The project should not open another internal public-artifact experiment merely to avoid the
owner gate.

If the sponsor reopens external contact, choose the external gate that matches the hypothesis
rather than treating "partner validation" as one undifferentiated exercise.
